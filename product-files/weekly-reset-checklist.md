# Weekly Reset Checklist
**Buzzyfly — buzzyfly.com**

Run this every Monday (or your first working day of the week).
Total time: 20 minutes.

---

## Minutes 1–5: Clear

Scan every place work comes in:
- [ ] Email inbox — move anything actionable to your task system. Archive the rest.
- [ ] Voicemail / texts — log any action items
- [ ] Notes app / sticky notes — anything you jotted down last week
- [ ] Slack / messaging apps — flag anything unresolved

**Goal:** Nothing sitting in an inbox that should be in your task list.

---

## Minutes 5–12: Review

Open your task system and go through everything:
- [ ] Mark anything that's actually done as done
- [ ] Note anything that slipped from last week
- [ ] Flag anything that's now overdue
- [ ] Remove anything that's no longer relevant

**Ask yourself:** What's actually on my plate right now?
