# Follow-Up Email Templates
**Buzzyfly — buzzyfly.com**

5 ready-to-send templates. Fill in the brackets, hit send.
Each template is intentionally short — that's by design.

---

## Template 1: Proposal Follow-Up
**When:** 5–7 days after sending a proposal, if you haven't heard back.

---

**Subject:** Re: [Project name] proposal

Hi [Name],

Just checking in on the proposal I sent over on [date]. Happy to answer any questions or adjust anything if the scope isn't quite right.

Let me know either way — completely fine if the timing isn't right.

[Your name]

---

## Template 2: Mid-Project Check-In
**When:** Midway through a project, or if things have gone quiet for a week.

---

**Subject:** Quick check-in — [project name]

Hi [Name],

Just checking in on [project/work]. How's everything looking on your end?

Anything you need from me, or any questions before we get to [next milestone]?

[Your name]

---

## Template 3: Re-Engagement (Lead Gone Quiet)
**When:** A lead said "reach out in a few weeks" and you haven't heard back, or a conversation went cold.

---

**Subject:** Still open if the timing's right

Hi [Name],

We talked about [brief context — e.g., "working together on your website redesign"] back in [month]. I know things get busy — just wanted to check in to see if it's worth reconnecting.

If the timing isn't right, no problem at all. If it is, happy to pick up where we left off.

[Your name]

---

## Template 4: Referral Request
**When:** A project wrapped up well and you want to ask for referrals. Send within 2 weeks of finishing.

---

**Subject:** Quick favor

Hi [Name],

Really glad [project outcome — e.g., "the launch went smoothly / the site is live"]. It was a good project.

If you know anyone who could use [what you do], I'd be grateful for the introduction. You know the kind of work I do — your word carries a lot more weight than any pitch I could make.

No pressure either way. Thanks for working with me.

[Your name]

---

## Template 5: End-of-Project Wrap-Up
**When:** A project is finished. Closes the loop and plants the seed for future work.

---

**Subject:** [Project name] — all done

Hi [Name],

Everything for [project] is wrapped up on my end. [One sentence summary of what was delivered — e.g., "The final files are in the shared folder / the designs are in Figma."]

It was genuinely a good project to work on. If anything comes up where I can help again — new project, a question, or a referral — don't hesitate to reach out.

[Your name]

---

## How to Use These

1. When a follow-up is due, open this file
2. Pick the right template
3. Replace the brackets — name, project, date
4. Read it once before sending — adjust tone if needed
5. Send

**That's it.** No staring at a blank screen.

---

**Log every follow-up** in one place (a notes app, your task manager, a spreadsheet) with:
- Who it's for
- What it's about
- When to send it

Check that list every morning. 5 minutes. Nothing falls through.
