# Client Onboarding Kit
**Buzzyfly — buzzyfly.com**

Everything you need to onboard a new client in 20 minutes.
Trigger: new contract signed / deposit received.

---

## Step 1: Send the Welcome Email (3 minutes)
**Send within 1 hour of confirming the client.**

---

**Subject:** Welcome — here's what happens next

Hi [Name],

Really glad we're working together on [project/service].

Here's what the next few steps look like:

1. I'll send you a short intake form today — it takes about 10 minutes and helps me hit the ground running
2. Once I have that back, I'll set up [shared folder / project space] and send you the link
3. Our kickoff call is [date/time] — I'll send a calendar invite shortly

If anything comes up before then, the best way to reach me is [email/phone].

Looking forward to it.

[Your name]

---

## Step 2: Send the Intake Form
**Send the same day as the welcome email.**

### Intake Questions (copy these into your form tool — Typeform, Google Forms, or just reply by email)

**About the project:**
1. In your own words, what does success look like at the end of this project?
2. What's the single most important outcome for you?
3. Is there anything you've tried before that didn't work? What happened?

**About working together:**
4. How do you prefer to communicate — email, calls, text, or a project tool?
5. How often do you want updates from me?
6. Who else (if anyone) needs to be looped in on decisions?

**Practical:**
7. Are there any hard deadlines I should know about?
8. Anything else I should know before we start?

---

**48-hour follow-up if no intake response:**

> Hi [Name], just following up on the intake form I sent yesterday — let me know if it didn't come through or if you have questions. Happy to do a quick call instead if that's easier.

---

## Step 3: Set Up the Shared Space (2 minutes)
**Create before the kickoff call — even if it's empty.**

Name the folder/project: `[ClientName] — [Project] — [Year]`

Add to it:
- [ ] A folder called "From [Client Name]" (for anything they send you)
- [ ] A folder called "Working Files"
- [ ] A folder called "Deliverables"

Share the link in a quick email:
> Hi [Name], I've set up our shared [folder/space] here: [link]. Everything we work on together will live there.

---

## Step 4: Create the Kickoff Agenda
**Send 24 hours before the call.**

---

**Subject:** Kickoff call agenda — [date]

Hi [Name],

Looking forward to our call tomorrow at [time]. Here's what I'd like to cover:

1. Quick intro / catch-up (5 min)
2. Walk through your intake answers — make sure I understood them correctly (10 min)
3. Confirm the project scope, timeline, and key dates (10 min)
4. How we'll work together — communication, approvals, check-ins (5 min)
5. Questions from you (5 min)

Should be about 30–40 minutes total.

[Video/call link]

[Your name]

---

## Step 5: Create the Internal Client Note
**Create before or right after the kickoff call.**

Copy this template into your notes app / task system:

```
CLIENT: [Full name]
COMPANY: [If applicable]
EMAIL: [Primary email]
PHONE: [If applicable]

SERVICE: [What you're doing for them]
START DATE: [Date]
END DATE / DEADLINE: [Date]
VALUE: [$amount]

BILLING: [How/when you invoice — e.g., "50% upfront, 50% on delivery"]
INVOICE DATES: [Dates]

KEY CONTACTS: [Anyone else involved]

NOTES:
[Anything from intake or kickoff that will matter later]

FOLLOW-UPS SCHEDULED:
- [Date]: [What]
```
