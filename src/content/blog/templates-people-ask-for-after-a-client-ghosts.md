A client says they are in. Then nothing. You rewrite the same email three times, rebuild the intake from memory, and the week dies in your inbox. Silence is not a maybe. Silence is an open file with no next date.

These are the five templates people ask for after that happens. Copy the lines. Send them on the day, not when you feel brave. If you want the files already written, the Digital System is the $49 checkout.

## The rule before any template

One thread. One ask. One date. If you cannot name the date you will close the file, do not send the note. A follow-up with no close date trains them to ignore you.

Write this on the proposal the day you send it:

```
Sent: ________
Day 3 bump: ________
Day 6 yes/later/no: ________
Day 12 close: ________
Waiting on: them / me
```

If that card is not on the calendar, Thursday will erase the client.

## 1. Day 3 — bump, not a new pitch

Send this on day 3. Not day 1. Not a longer proposal.

Subject: Proposal from [date] — one question

Hi [name] — the proposal for [service] is still the one I sent [date]. One question: is [start date] still the date, or did it move?

Reply with the date that works. If it is dead, reply NO and I close it.

Time box: 3 minutes. Do not attach the proposal again unless they ask. A second PDF looks like you do not trust the first one.

## 2. Day 6 — yes, later, or no

Half of them will still be quiet. That is the job. Send this once.

Subject: Still good for [service]?

Hi [name] — checking the proposal I sent [date]. If it is a yes, reply YES and I send the intake today. If timing moved, reply LATER and a date. If it is a no, reply NO and I close the file. Either way I stop guessing.

Time box: 4 minutes. One send. No second rewrite in the same hour.

## 3. Day 12 — close the file

Use this when they opened the email and still did not answer. Do not send a fourth version of the offer.

Subject: Closing this Friday

Hi [name] — I am closing open proposals Friday at 4. If you still want [service] starting [date], reply YES before then. If not, no reply needed. I will not chase this thread after Friday.

That line does the deciding for them. A closed file is worth more than a maybe that eats Monday.

Paste log after you send it:

```
Closed: ________
Reason: no reply / later on [date] / no
Next name to contact: ________
```

## 4. Intake that starts the job — only after YES

Do not start work on a verbal yes. Start on these five fields. Send them the same day they reply YES.

- Name and the person who approves
- Service they bought, in their words
- Start date and the first deliverable
- How they want updates (email, text, one weekly note)
- What is out of scope

If field 5 is blank, the job will sprawl. Ask it before kickoff.

Kickoff note, 6 lines, send with the intake:

> You are in. First deliverable is [one thing] on [date].
> I need [one file, login, or decision] by [date].
> Kickoff is [day, time]. Agenda: outcome, what is out, first deliverable, next check-in.
> Reply if that slot does not work.

One deliverable. One ask. A reply is the lock-in. If you cannot name the next date, you do not have onboarding. You have a paid task sitting in your head.

## 5. Scope reply when they add work mid-silence

The ghost often comes back with “quick question” that is a new job. Do not answer the new job inside the old thread as a favor.

Client: can you also do X?

You: X is outside the signed scope. I can add it as a separate line at [price] and start after you reply YES. The current job stays on the original date.

Do not say yes in the same breath you explain the price. If they vanished before paying, do not scope the extra until the original proposal has a YES and a date.

## Monday reset so the list does not die by Thursday

20 minutes. Before email. Same window every week.

1. Clear anything under 2 minutes.
2. Write three priorities. Not ten. If a fourth appears, one of the three leaves.
3. List who is waiting on you and who you are waiting on.
4. Schedule the day-3, day-6, and day-12 notes that are due. If it is not on a calendar block, it is not a follow-up.
5. Write tomorrow's first move in one line.

Fill-in for the quiet clients:

```
Name: ________
Last touch: ________
Next touch: day 3 / 6 / 12 on ________
Close date if no reply: ________
```

If this list lives only in your head, the ghosted client stays ghosted.
