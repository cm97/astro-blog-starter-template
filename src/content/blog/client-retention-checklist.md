Churn isn't a surprise. It's a slow leak you ignored.

## The five touchpoints

1. **Day 7 check-in:** "How's the first week going? Anything I can fix?"
2. **Mid-project pulse:** A quick survey or call. Catch problems early.
3. **Delivery celebration:** Acknowledge the win. Even a short message.
4. **30-day review:** What worked, what didn't, what's next.
5. **Referral ask:** After a win, ask for one introduction. Not a testimonial — a name.

## The rule

Every touchpoint has a date. If it's not on the calendar, it doesn't happen. The Digital System ships this as a retention cadence checklist with email templates for each touchpoint.
