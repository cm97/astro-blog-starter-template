Friday afternoon. Delivery is done. The open invoices from last week are still sitting. You tell yourself you will chase them Monday. Monday arrives and the new week already has three new fires. The cash that should have landed never does.

This 10-minute close runs every Friday before 3pm. Same words. Same log. Cash moves or the file closes.

## The 10-minute Friday invoice close

Stop at ten minutes even if you have more invoices. The ones that leave today are the ones that still get paid this weekend or Monday.

1. **Open the private note (2 min).** List every invoice older than the due date. Amount, due date, last reminder sent.
2. **Day-1 or Day-7 reminder (4 min).** One subject, four lines. Paste the exact email below.
3. **Log the send (1 min).** Same note. Next touch is Monday or the date they name.
4. **Close the tab (1 min).** If no reply by Monday 11am, mark quiet and move to the day-14 close. Do not invent a third soft nudge.
5. **Count the cash (2 min).** Write the total still open. That number is the only one that matters.

Fill this before you open any draft:

```
Invoice: ________
Client: ________
Amount: $________
Due: ________
Last reminder: ________
Friday close sent: ________ (time)
Reply logged: yes / no
Next action: paid / day-14 / closed
```

Phone-paste version:

> Invoice [number] for [amount] due [date]. Still the right week, or date shifted?

If this is already day 7 or later, swap the second line for:

> This is the day-7 reminder. Reply with a new date today or the file moves to the final close Monday.

## What you do when they reply

- **Paying today / this weekend:** reply with the payment link the same hour. Log the paid date.
- **New date:** one line: “Understood. Holding until [specific day]. No further reminders until then.” Do not offer a free extension past that day.
- **Quiet:** wait until Monday 11am. If still silent, send the day-14 close from the earlier post and mark the original date dead.
- **Scope or extra ask inside the reply:** answer with the two-option line. Do not start extra work in this thread.

## Extra lines that stop the invoice from dying over the weekend

- Send from the same address that sent the original invoice. A new thread from a different account gets ignored.
- Put a 10-minute calendar block titled “Friday invoice close” every Thursday night. The block is the trigger.
- Keep the original due date in the private note. If you cannot paste it in one line, do not send the reminder yet.
- One question only. A second question turns the email into a form they never finish.
- If the amount is already late, name the new date in the same email. Do not ask them to suggest one.
- Log the send time in the note before you close the tab. The Monday reset finds the date or the cash disappears.
- Cap the whole sequence at two touches before the final close. A third soft email trains them to ignore you.
- Never discount in the reminder. The original amount stays the record.
- After you send, set a no-check rule until Monday. Looking Saturday turns the close into another wait.
- At the end of the 10 minutes, write the total still open on the Monday still-open line. Zero open ghosts is the goal.
