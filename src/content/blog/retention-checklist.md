Acquisition is expensive. Retention is cheap. Most solo operators ignore the second one.

## The retention checklist

1. **Day-after check-in** — "How's it going?" Two sentences. Builds trust.
2. **30-day value drop** — send something useful with no ask.
3. **Quarterly review** — a short call or email: what worked, what's next.
4. **Referral ask** — only after they've seen results. "Know anyone who'd benefit?"
5. **Birthday/anniversary note** — human, not automated spam.
