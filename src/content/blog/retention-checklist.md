Acquiring a new client costs 5 to 7 times more than keeping an existing one.

Most solo operators know this. Almost none of them have a retention system. They deliver the work, send the final files, and then go silent until they need more revenue.

The client fills the void with whoever shows up next.

## What retention actually requires

Not a CRM. Not a newsletter. Not a loyalty program.

Just contact. Consistent, professional, human contact at the right moments.

A Friday update during an active project. A two-week check-in after delivery. A testimonial ask 30 days out. A referral ask at 60 days. A quarterly re-engagement email to everyone who's gone quiet.

That's the whole system. Most people just don't run it consistently.

## The five phases

The Buzzyfly retention checklist covers the full client lifecycle:

- **During the project** — specific weekly touchpoints that reduce anxiety and build trust while the work is in progress
- **At delivery** — the exact email structure that turns a handoff into a lasting impression
- **Post-delivery** — a four-touch sequence at 2 weeks, 30 days, 60 days, and 90 days, including the testimonial ask and the referral ask word-for-word
- **Building a referral system** — how to ask for referrals in a way that's easy to answer and doesn't feel awkward
- **Quarterly retention review** — a 15-minute review to find the clients who've gone quiet and get back in front of them

Every section includes the email template you can send the same day.
