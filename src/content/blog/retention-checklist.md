# Retention Checklist

This is the actual checklist. Not a summary. The steps.

## During the project

- [ ] Send a progress update every Friday, even if nothing changed
- [ ] Ask one question they didn't expect — shows you're paying attention
- [ ] Deliver one small win early, before the big deliverable
- [ ] Never let 7 days pass without contact

## After delivery

- [ ] Send a "how's it going" email 2 weeks later
- [ ] Ask for a testimonial while they're happy
- [ ] Offer one free thing — a tip, a template, a quick review
- [ ] Put them on a quarterly check-in list

## The rule

Retention is not a campaign. It's a habit. One email a month keeps you top of mind.
