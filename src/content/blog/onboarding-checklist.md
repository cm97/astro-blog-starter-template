Most onboarding dies in your head. You meet a new client, feel the rush, scribble notes, and by Thursday the whole thing is a blur. This checklist fixes that.

## The 7-step onboarding that actually sticks

1. **Welcome email** — sent within 1 hour of payment. Sets expectations, links to the intake form.
2. **Intake form** — 5 questions max. Name, goal, deadline, budget, one thing they hate about past providers.
3. **Kickoff call** — 20 minutes. Confirm the goal, agree on the first deliverable, set the next check-in.
4. **First deliverable** — shipped within 48 hours. Small win builds trust.
5. **Weekly check-in** — same day, same time. 10 minutes. Status, blocker, next step.
6. **Scope guard** — one line in every email: "Anything outside this is a new project."
7. **Close-out** — summary of what was done, what worked, what to repeat next time.

Run this every time. It takes 20 minutes to set up once, then it runs itself.
