Most proposals are novels nobody reads. This one is a page they actually finish.

## The structure

**Section 1 — The problem (their words):** Restate what they told you in discovery. Make them feel heard.

**Section 2 — The outcome:** What changes for them in 30/60/90 days. Specific. Not vague.

**Section 3 — The plan:** Three to five steps. Dated. No fluff.

**Section 4 — The investment:** Price, what's included, what's not. One clean number.

**Section 5 — The next step:** A single action. "Reply yes and I'll send the kickoff date."

## Why it works

Because it removes friction. The client doesn't have to imagine the outcome — it's written. The Digital System has this as an editable template with a pricing calculator baked in.
