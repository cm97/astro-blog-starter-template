Discovery calls fail when you talk too much. These questions flip it.

## The opener

1. What's the one thing that made you reach out today?
2. What have you already tried?
3. What would success look like in 90 days?

## The money questions

4. What's the budget range you're comfortable with?
5. What happens if this doesn't get solved?
6. Who else is involved in the decision?

## The pain questions

7. What's the most frustrating part of the current situation?
8. How much time or money is this costing you weekly?
9. What would you do with the time you get back?

## The close questions

10. What would make you say yes right now?
11. What would make you say no?
12. When do you want to start?
