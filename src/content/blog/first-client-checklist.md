You don't need a perfect brand. You need a checklist.

## Day 1 — Pick your offer

One sentence. One outcome. One price. Done.

## Day 2 — Find 10 people

Not strangers. People you already know. Message them directly.

## Day 3 — Send the discovery questions

Use the 12-question sheet. Listen more than you talk.

## Day 4 — Send the proposal

One page. Problem, outcome, plan, price, next step.

## Day 5 — Follow up

Day 0, Day 3, Day 7. No limbo.

## Day 6 — Close

Ask for the yes. Or the no. Either is fine.

## Day 7 — Deliver the first win

Even a small one. Momentum beats perfection.

## The rule

Every step has a date. The Digital System ships this as a 7-day sprint checklist with templates for each day.
