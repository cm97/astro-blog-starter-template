These templates are for coaches. Paste them. Fill the blanks. Send them on the clock, not when you feel ready.

## Post-session recap (send within 2 hours)

The recap does three jobs: locks the actions while the session is fresh, shows you were listening, and gives the client a document they can open later without asking you to repeat yourself.

Subject: Your session recap + next steps

Hi [Name],

Here is what we covered today and the actions we agreed on.

Key points:
- [One insight or decision]
- [Second]
- [Third]

Your actions this week:
1. [Specific action] — by [date]
2. [Specific action] — by [date]

I will check in on [day] about the first one. Reply if anything needs adjusting before then.

[Your name]

Send it before you close the laptop. If the session ends at 3pm, the recap is out by 5pm. The later it goes, the more you forget the exact wording they used.

## 48-hour check-in (send 2 days later)

This is not a sales email. It is proof you remember what they said they would do.

Subject: Quick check on [specific action]

Hi [Name],

Checking in on the [specific action from the recap]. How did that go?

No long reply needed. A one-line update is fine so I know where we stand before the next session.

[Your name]

Put the date on your calendar the minute you send the recap. If you wait until you "have a moment," the moment is after the next client and the check-in never leaves.

## Discovery call follow-up (send the same day or next morning)

You just finished the call. They are still thinking about the problem you named. Do not wait three days.

Subject: Next steps after our call — [their goal in 4 words]

Hi [Name],

Good talking today about [the specific problem they named].

Based on what you said, the next step is [one clear option: a paid session, a short proposal, or a decision by [date]].

I can start [date] if that works. Reply with yes, a different date, or "not now" and I will close the loop.

[Your name]

One option. One date. An easy no. "Let me know what you think" is not a next step. It is a request for them to do your job.

## Quiet lead (21+ days, or a discovery that went silent)

Use this once. Not a weekly drip.

Subject: Checking back — [the problem you discussed]

Hi [Name],

We talked in [month] about [the exact problem]. Timing was not right then.

Is that still something you are working on, or has it sorted itself out?

Either way is fine. Just wanted to check once.

[Your name]

Cap this at one quiet lead per week. Five of them in an afternoon turns into spam you do not want to send.

## Break-up for a proposal that stayed quiet (about day 14)

Subject: Closing the file on [project or package name]

Hi [Name],

I am going to assume the [package or proposal] is not the right time and close the file on my side.

If that is wrong, reply this week and I will reopen it. If not, no hard feelings.

[Your name]

This is the email people actually answer. It creates a real deadline without a fake countdown.
