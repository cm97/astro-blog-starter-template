## Rules before you paste anything

- One email. One question. One next step.
- Use their project name in the first line.
- Give them a clean out. People reply to easy exits.
- Do not apologize for existing.
- Send Tuesday or Wednesday morning. Skip Friday.
- Stop after the break-up email. Chasing after that is unpaid work.

## Email 1 — The bump (day 3)

**Subject:** Re: [project name] proposal

> Hi [Name],
>
> Wanted to make sure the [project name] proposal did not get buried.
>
> Still want to move forward, or should I take it off my list?
>
> Happy to answer anything that is blocking the decision.
>
> [Your name]

Job: confirm they saw it. Not a novel. Not a second pitch.

## Email 2 — The value bump (day 7)

**Subject:** One note on [project name]

> Hi [Name],
>
> One thing that usually trips this kind of project: [one specific risk you already discussed].
>
> If that is still the blocker, I can adjust scope before you decide.
>
> If timing is off, say so and I will close the file.
>
> [Your name]

Job: add one useful line they did not have last week. Do not attach a 20-page PDF.

## Email 3 — The decision email (day 10)

**Subject:** Need a yes/no on [project name]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I need a yes or a no on the [project name] proposal so I can lock next month.
>
> If you want changes, send the two that matter and I will revise once.
>
> If it is a no, that is useful too.
>
> [Your name]

Job: force a decision. Soft check-ins keep the deal in limbo and keep you unpaid.

## Email 4 — Quiet-lead poke (30–90 days later)

**Subject:** Checking back on [the problem]

> Hi [Name],
>
> We talked in [month] about [the problem]. Timing was off then.
>
> Is that still on your plate, or did it get solved another way?
>
> Either answer is fine.
>
> [Your name]

Job: reopen dead pipeline without begging. Use this on old calls, not on last week's quote.

## Email 5 — The break-up (day 14 on a live quote)

**Subject:** Closing the file on [project name]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I am going to assume [project name] is not the right time and close the file on my side.
>
> If that is wrong, reply this week and I will reopen it.
>
> If not, no hard feelings. You know where to find me later.
>
> [Your name]

Job: create a deadline without a fake countdown. This is the email people actually answer.

## The 20-minute weekly habit that makes these send

Templates do not matter if they live in a notes app you never open.

Every Monday, 20 minutes:

1. List every proposal with no reply.
2. Mark day-count: 3 / 7 / 10 / 14.
3. Paste the matching email. Fill two fields. Send.
4. Move silent 14-day files to closed unless they reply.
5. Put one quiet-lead poke on the calendar for a 30-day-old conversation.

That is the whole follow-up system. Not a CRM rebuild. Not a new app.
