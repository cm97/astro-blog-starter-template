You sent the proposal. You waited. Nothing. You sent a reminder. Nothing. You gave up. The client did not say no. They just forgot. And you let them.

Follow-ups are not annoying. They are the difference between a quote and a closed deal. Most freelancers send one email and call it done. That is not a follow-up. That is a hope.

## Template 1: The bump (day 3)

"Hey [name] — wanted to make sure this didn't get buried. Still interested in moving forward? Happy to answer any questions."

Short. No pressure. One question.

## Template 2: The value add (day 7)

"Hey [name] — found this [resource] that might help with [their problem]. Thought of you. Still happy to help if you want to move forward."

Give something. Remind them you exist. Do not beg.

## Template 3: The break-up (day 14)

"Hey [name] — I am going to assume this isn't the right time. No worries. If it changes, you know where to find me."

This one works because it creates urgency without pressure. People reply to break-ups. They ignore nudges.

## Why this works

It is a system, not a feeling. You are not hoping they remember. You are running a sequence that makes forgetting impossible.
