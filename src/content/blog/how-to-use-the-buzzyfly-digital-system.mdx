You paid for the system. Now don't let it rot in your Downloads folder like every other template you bought in a fit of optimism.

## What's actually in the zip

Open it and you'll find a small, boring, beautiful folder:

- `README.md` — the map. Read this first.
- `01-onboarding/` — the onboarding checklist, intake form and kickoff call agenda.
- `02-weekly-planning/` — the 20-minute weekly reset, Monday setup and Friday review.
- `03-follow-ups/` — the follow-up emails, the cadence and the breakup email.
- `04-scope-and-proposals/` — the scope-creep script, proposal template and pricing worksheet.
- `05-retention/` — the retention checklist and re-engagement email.

The [full file list](/blog/product-contents/) shows every file by name.

No 200-tab spreadsheet. No course with 40 videos you'll never watch. Just files you can run this week.

## The 5-minute setup

1. **Pick one checklist.** Start with onboarding — it's the one that saves you the most pain fastest.
2. **Print it or pin it.** Phone notes, a printed sheet, whatever you'll actually see. Out of sight is out of system.
3. **Run it on the next real client.** Don't wait for a perfect week. The checklist exists for the messy ones.
4. **Tweak it once.** After one real run, cross out what didn't fit and add what did. Now it's yours.

## When it breaks

It will. You'll forget a step, a client will throw a curveball, the week will eat you. That's fine — the system is the floor, not the ceiling. The point isn't perfection. It's that the process still exists on the weeks you are slammed.
