A client texts at 9:47pm: "Can you just add this one thing before tomorrow?" You are half-asleep. The yes leaves before the price does. By morning the extra work is free and the original deadline is already late.

That is the failure. The reply lives in your head. Night erases the price.

Send this instead. Four minutes. Same words every time. The file stays closed until a written yes lands.

## The 4-minute log before you reply

Open the same note as your Monday reset. Fill this block.

```
Client: ________
Ask: ________
Time received: ________
Minutes to do it: ________
Line-item price: $________
Two-option sent: yes / no
Status: waiting / yes / parked
```

If the minutes or the price are blank, do not reply with a yes. The pause is the gate.

## Exact reply — send from your phone

> Got the note on [ask]. Outside the locked scope. Two options by [time tomorrow]: add it as a line item at [price], or park it until after [original delivery]. Not starting it tonight.

Phone version under 160 characters:

> Got [ask]. Outside scope. Two options tomorrow: add at [price] or park. Not starting tonight.

No apology. No "happy to help." One price. One choice. The night ends with the file still closed.

## What you do the minute it leaves

1. Mark the status waiting in the log.
2. Do not open the file. Do not start the work.
3. Set a phone reminder for 9am titled "Scope reply follow-up — 2 min".
4. If they reply yes overnight, still wait until morning to confirm in writing and invoice the line.
5. On the next weekly reset, move every waiting scope ask into the open list or mark it parked.
