Most solo operators price from fear: "What will they say no to?" That's not pricing. That's hoping.

## The 3-number method

1. **Your monthly number** — rent, food, tools, the bare minimum to not panic.
2. **Billable hours** — how many hours you can actually sell in a month (usually less than you think).
3. **Price = monthly number ÷ billable hours**, then add 20% for the work you hate.

Example: $6,000 / 80 hours = $75/hour. Add 20% = $90. That's your floor, not your ceiling.
