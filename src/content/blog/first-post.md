Most business advice sounds the same after a while: get organized, be consistent, follow up faster. All true, and all pretty useless without something to hold that advice in place once the week actually gets busy.

I built the Buzzyfly Digital System because I kept doing the same thing to myself — setting up a clean process on a slow Monday, feeling great about it, and watching it fall apart by Thursday when three things happened at once. The problem was never motivation. It was that nothing in my setup survived contact with an actual busy week.

So instead of another productivity app or a 200-tab template pack, I built the small number of workflows that actually matter most for a one- or few-person business:

- **Onboarding**, so a new client doesn't mean rebuilding the same steps from memory.
- **Weekly planning**, so priorities live somewhere other than your head.
- **Follow-ups**, so nothing quietly dies in your inbox.

Each one is built to still work on the days you don't feel like running it — because those are exactly the days it matters most.

That's the whole pitch, really. Not "do more," just "stop losing what you already built." If that sounds familiar, the Digital System is below.
