Most weekly planning systems fail for one reason: they take too long. You spend an hour on Sunday building a beautiful plan, and by Wednesday it's a joke.

The fix is a reset that takes 20 minutes and actually survives.

## The 20-minute reset

1. **List the 3 things that must move this week.** Not 10. Three.
2. **Check the inbox for anything dying.** Follow-ups, unanswered questions, stalled clients.
3. **Pick one client to onboard or check in with.** Don't let onboarding pile up.
4. **Block the time.** Put the 3 things on the calendar with real hours.
5. **Close the list.** Write it down. Out of sight is out of system.

## The rule

If it takes more than 20 minutes, it's not a reset — it's a project. Keep it short enough that you'll actually do it on the weeks you're slammed.
