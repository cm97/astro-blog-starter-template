## What each folder does

**01-onboarding** covers signed contract to first deliverable. The checklist, the [intake form](/blog/client-intake-form-template/), and the [kickoff call agenda](/blog/client-kickoff-call-agenda/).

**02-weekly-planning** is the [20-minute weekly reset](/blog/weekly-reset/). Monday setup and the Friday review. Short and repeatable.

**03-follow-ups** holds the email templates, the cadence for when to send them, and the breakup email for a lead that has gone quiet for good. The approach is the same as in [follow-up email after no response](/blog/follow-up-email-after-no-response/).

**04-scope-and-proposals** has the words for saying no to extra work ([scope creep script](/blog/scope-creep-script/)), a one-page [proposal template](/blog/proposal-that-closes/), and the [pricing worksheet](/blog/pricing-your-time-without-guessing/).

**05-retention** keeps a finished client from going quiet. The [retention checklist](/blog/retention-checklist/) and the re-engagement email.

## How to start

1. Download the zip and unzip it.
2. Read the README first. It is the map.
3. Pick one checklist and run it on a real client this week.
4. Don't try to run everything at once. That is how systems die.

The longer walkthrough is in [how to use the Buzzyfly Digital System](/blog/how-to-use-the-buzzyfly-digital-system/).
