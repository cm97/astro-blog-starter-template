## What you download

```
Buzzyfly Client OS/

├── How to open.txt
```

It works in Excel 2016 or newer and Microsoft 365, on Windows and Mac. It also works in Google Sheets: upload the file and everything keeps working. There are no macros, so there are no security warnings to click through.

Yellow cells are for you to type in. Grey cells calculate themselves and are locked, so you can't break a formula by accident.

## The tabs

**Today.** Open the workbook and it shows how many follow-ups are due, who they are, which email to send each one, which onboarding steps are due, and this week's #1 priority.

**Follow-Ups.** Add every lead, proposal and client you're waiting on. Pick their stage and the date you last contacted them. The workbook works out the next follow-up date and which email to send. Leads get a nudge on day 3, something useful on day 7, and a polite close on day 14. Proposals get follow-ups on days 3, 7, 12 and 18. Past clients get a check-in at two weeks, a testimonial ask at 30 days and a referral ask at 60. Rows turn red when someone is due.

**Email Writer.** Pick a person and one of 30 emails. The workbook fills in their first name, their project and your sign-off. Copy the lines and paste them into your email.

**Onboarding.** One row per new client. Enter the date they signed and the kickoff call, and the workbook lays out their first ten days: welcome email, intake form, shared folder, kickoff agenda, kickoff summary and first-week check-ins. It tracks progress and shows the next step and its email.

**Intake Form.** Edit the questions you send every new client, then paste the numbered form into Google Forms, Typeform or an email.

**Weekly Reset and Weekly Log.** A guided 20-minute Monday plan. Score what's fighting for first place and the workbook picks your #1 priority. Log each week's result and revenue, and watch the chart.

**Proposal.** Fill in a short form: their problem in their words, deliverables, milestones. The proposal writes itself, priced from your Pricing tab.

**Pricing.** Enter your income target and hours. Get your hourly floor, a project price and a retainer price.

**Scope Guard.** A client asks for more than they paid for. Pick what's happening and get the exact words to say, with two options and a path forward.

**Lead Scorer.** Pick what's true about a new lead and know how much energy to invest before the call.
