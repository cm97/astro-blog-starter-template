## What you download


Unzip it and double-click the app. It opens in your web browser. No install, no account, no internet connection needed. It works on Windows, Mac and Chromebook in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

## The screens

**Today.** Open the app and it shows who needs a follow-up today, which onboarding steps are due, and this week's #1 priority.

**Follow-ups.** Add every lead, proposal and client you're waiting on. The app works out the next follow-up date and which email to send. Leads get a nudge on day 3, something useful on day 7, and a polite close on day 14. Proposals get follow-ups on days 3, 7, 12 and 18. Past clients get a check-in at two weeks, a testimonial ask at 30 days and a referral ask at 60.

**Email writer.** 30 emails for every moment, from the proposal follow-up to the referral ask. Pick a person and the app fills in their name, their project and your sign-off. Fill in the yellow boxes, then copy the email or open it in your email app. Save your own versions as templates.

**Onboarding.** When a client signs, the app lays out their first ten days with due dates: welcome email, intake form, shared folder, kickoff agenda, kickoff summary and first-week check-ins. Client notes save as you type.

**Intake form.** Edit the questions you send every new client, then copy them into Google Forms, Typeform or an email.

**Weekly reset.** A guided 20-minute flow with a timer. Clear your inboxes, review your list, score what's fighting for first place, and pick one #1 priority. Thursday check-in, Friday close, and a history of your weeks and revenue.

**Scope guard.** A client asks for more than they paid for. Pick what's happening and get the exact words to say, with two options and a path forward.

**Proposals and pricing.** Enter your income target and hours to get your hourly floor, a project price and a retainer price. Then fill in a short form and the proposal writes itself.

**Lead scorer.** Tick what's true about a new lead and know how much energy to invest before the call.

## Your data

Everything you enter stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The app saves a backup file whenever you ask, so you can move to a new computer.
