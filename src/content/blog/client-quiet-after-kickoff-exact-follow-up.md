Kickoff ends. They say they will send the files. Then nothing. Three days. Five. The first deliverable date is still on your calendar and their inbox is quiet.

That silence is where week one dies. You wait for the “right” moment. The moment never comes. The project drifts. Cash sits.

Send these instead. Same lines every time. No drafting from a blank screen.

## Day 3 — the short poke

Subject: Quick check — [project name]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Checking that the files / access we named on the kickoff are on the way.
>
> First deliverable is still set for [date]. Reply with the link or a new date if something is blocking it.
>
> [Your name]

One ask. One date. Send it on day 3 even if you feel awkward.

## Day 7 — the decision line

Subject: Still holding [date] for [deliverable]?

> Hi [Name],
>
> Still holding [date] for the first deliverable, or should I move it so the slot is free?
>
> One line is enough. If the files are ready, reply with the link and we stay on the original date.
>
> [Your name]

This one forces a yes, a no, or a new date. Silence after this becomes a paused row, not an open one.

## If they reply with a delay

Write the new date on the same card the same day. Do not leave it in the thread. Then put the new day-3 and day-7 on the calendar again.
