You did the hard part. You got the client. You signed the contract. You sent the invoice. And then — week three — they go quiet. No replies. No feedback. No payment for the next phase.

You did not lose them because your work was bad. You lost them because you had no system for keeping them. Onboarding got them in the door. Retention keeps them from walking out.

## The retention checklist

**Week 1:** Send a welcome message. Not a form letter. One sentence about what you are excited to build together. Ask one question that shows you listened.

**Week 2:** Send a mid-project check-in. "How is this feeling so far? Anything you want to adjust?" This is not a status update. It is a pulse check. It catches problems before they become ghosting.

**Week 3:** Send a value recap. Three bullets of what you have delivered so far. Not a brag. A reminder. People forget what you did for them. Remind them.

**Week 4:** Send the next-step proposal. "Here is what comes next. Here is what it costs. Here is when we start." Do not wait for them to ask. Lead.

## Why this works

It turns retention from a feeling into a habit. You are not hoping they stay. You are running a system that makes staying the default.
