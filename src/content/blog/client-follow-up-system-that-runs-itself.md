You know the follow-up should go out. The proposal went quiet. The new client has not sent the intake. The past client has not heard from you in three months.

It does not go out because the week got loud. Delivery ate the morning. By Thursday the list lives only in your head, and heads drop things.

The fix is not more willpower. It is a system that still runs when you are slammed.

## The 20-minute weekly follow-up scan

Do this every Monday (or the first 20 minutes of your week). Same order every time.

1. Open the list of open proposals older than 5 days. Send the day-5/7 template if none exists.
2. Check new clients from the last 7 days. Confirm intake arrived. If not, send the intake nudge.
3. Scan active clients. Anyone quiet more than 7 days gets the mid-project check-in.
4. One past client from last quarter gets the re-engagement note.
5. Close the loop on anything older than 21 days with the break-up email.

That is the entire process. Five actions. Twenty minutes. The emails are already written.

## Exact emails that do the work

**Day 5–7 proposal follow-up**
Subject: Re: [Project] proposal

Hi [Name],

Following up on the proposal I sent [day] for [project].

Is this still something you want to move forward with, or should I take it off my plate?

Happy to adjust scope or timeline if anything changed.

[Your name]

**Intake missing after deposit**
Subject: Quick note before we start

Hi [Name],

Got the deposit. Ready to start on [date].

The intake form is the one thing I need from you so week one stays on track. Link is below. Takes about 8 minutes.

[link]

[Your name]

**Quiet mid-project**
Subject: Quick check-in — [project]

Hi [Name],

Quick check-in on [project]. We are at [stage] and on track from my side.

Anything you want to flag before [next date]?

[Your name]

These three cover 80% of the silence that kills cash.
