The number lives in your head. Delivery erases the calculation. Next proposal you guess again.

Run this worksheet before you send the proposal. Ten minutes. Same fields every time. If you only have five, stop after step 3.

## The 10-minute pricing worksheet

1. **Floor rate (2 minutes).** Annual income goal ÷ 50 weeks ÷ real billable hours per week. Not 40. The number you actually log. That is the floor. Never quote below it.
2. **Hours for this job (3 minutes).** Write the real tasks. Add 20% for the stuff you always forget. Cap the list at what ships in the first 30 days.
3. **The number (1 minute).** Rate × hours. Round up to the nearest $50. That is the quote. Not a range. Not “starting at.”
4. **Value check (2 minutes).** Is the number less than the outcome they named? If yes, raise it. If the outcome is vague, the price is not ready. Ask one clarifying question before you send.
5. **Scope guard (2 minutes).** Write what is out. One sentence. If they ask for it later, it is a new line item, not a free add-on.

Fill this before you open the proposal doc:

```
Annual goal: ________
Weeks: 50
Real billable hours/week: ________
Floor rate: $________/hr
Tasks for this job:
- ________ (hrs)
- ________ (hrs)
+ 20% buffer: ________ hrs
Quote: $________ (rounded up)
Outcome they named: ________
Out of scope: ________
```

## Worked example

- $80,000 ÷ 50 ÷ 25 billable hours = $64/hour floor.
- Tasks: discovery call 1h, outline 2h, draft 6h, revisions 2h. Subtotal 11h + 20% = 13.2h → 14h.
- 14 × $64 = $896. Round up to $900.
- Outcome: “clearer positioning so I can raise my own rates.” $900 is less than that. Keep it.
- Out of scope: ongoing social posts, redesign of their site, weekly calls after the first month.

Quote $900. One sentence: “$900 for the positioning workshop, outline, and first-draft rewrite. Anything after the first 30 days is a new quote.”

## Exact phrases when they push back

- “Can you do $600?” → “The floor for this scope is $900. I can cut the rewrite and quote the outline only at $450.”
- “Starting at?” → “The number for the scope above is $900. I do not do ranges.”
- “What if it takes longer?” → “The buffer is already in. Extra hours are a new line item at the same rate.”

Do not invent a discount in the moment. The worksheet already decided.
