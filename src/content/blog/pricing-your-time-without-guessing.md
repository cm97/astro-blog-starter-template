You quote $500. They say yes. You feel sick because you know it should have been $1,200. You quote $1,200. They say no. You feel sick because you know it should have been $500. You are pricing from anxiety, not from math.

Pricing is not a feeling. It is a calculation. And the calculation is simple.

## The worksheet

**Step 1: Your hourly rate.** What do you want to make per hour? Be honest. Not what you think you are worth. What you need to live.

**Step 2: Estimated hours.** How long will this actually take? Add 20% for the stuff you always forget.

**Step 3: The number.** Rate times hours. That is your price. Not a range. Not "starting at." The number.

**Step 4: The value check.** Is this price less than the value you are creating for them? If yes, you are undercharging. Raise it. If no, you are overcharging. Lower it or walk away.

## Why this works

It removes the guesswork. You are not hoping the number feels right. You are running a formula. The formula does not care about your anxiety.
