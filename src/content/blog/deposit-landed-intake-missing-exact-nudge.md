The deposit cleared. You sent the welcome and the intake link. Then nothing. The kickoff date is three days out and the form is still empty.

You do not want to chase a paid client. You also do not want the first week to start with a blank agenda and a private note that only says "paid."

These two lines pull the intake back without a novel. Run them on a fixed cadence so the onboarding does not die in the gap between money and information.

## The 2-minute log the minute the deposit lands

Copy this the same hour payment clears.

```
Client: ________
Deposit cleared: ________ (date / time)
Welcome + intake sent: ________
Kickoff booked: ________
Intake due: ________ (3 business days)
Day 2 nudge due: ________
Day 4 nudge due: ________
Status: waiting / received / closed
Next action: ________
```

Two minutes. Do it while the Stripe notification is still open. Memory of “they will fill it later” is not a date.

Put two 4-minute blocks on the calendar labeled “Intake nudge: [name] day 2/4”. If either lands on Friday, move it to Thursday morning.

## Day 2 — One-line reminder with the deadline restated

Send on day 2, morning before 11am. Mid-week preferred.

> Still need the short intake so the kickoff is useful. Link again: [link]. Deadline stays [date].

Subject: Intake still needed for [project] kickoff

One sentence. The link and the original deadline do the work. Do not add a second paragraph.

After send: update log with time. Do not check for 24 hours.

## Day 4 — Binary hold or reschedule

Still empty:

> Kickoff is on [day/time]. If the intake is not back by tomorrow morning I will move it to [next open slot]. Reply with the form or a new date that works.

Still one short block. Gives them a clear choice instead of silence.

Same window: before 11am. Same subject line.

After send: log the time. If they return the form, mark received and open the private note the same hour. If they name a new date, update the calendar and close the nudge blocks.

## What you do when they reply

- Form back: open the private note, write the outcome they named, send the kickoff summary template within the hour. Mark status received. Delete remaining nudge blocks.
- New date: update the calendar hold. Send one confirmation: “Moved kickoff to [new day/time]. Intake deadline stays [original].” Do not restart the clock.
- “Busy this week”: park until the date they name. One note. Do not hold the original kickoff.
- Silence after day 4: move the kickoff yourself. Send the one-line confirmation of the new time. Cap at two nudges. A third becomes noise.

## Extra steps that keep week one from dying

1. Paste the intake link into the log the same minute you send the welcome. If the link is not written, you cannot nudge.
2. Keep the two nudge lines in the same note as the welcome email. One open, both ready.
3. Cap at two emails. A third after day 4 turns into collection tone on a paid client.
4. After the form arrives, write the first-week goal into the private note before you close the laptop. Blank goal is how day 7 dies.
5. On the next Monday reset, add a row: “Intakes still open past day 2.” If none, write none.
6. Set the phone reminder for the 4-minute block the night before. Calendar alone fails when delivery is on fire.
7. If the original kickoff date has already passed by day 4, replace it with the next real slot you can hold before you send the line.
8. Never start delivery work while the intake is blank. The sequence exists so you do not decide that under pressure.

## Why these work when “just checking if you got the form” fails

“Just checking in” asks them to remember and decide. These restate the deadline and give one binary. The date makes the decision time-bound. You stop rebuilding the chase from memory every time a paid client goes quiet on the intake.
