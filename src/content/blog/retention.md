Getting a client is hard. Keeping one is a checklist.

## The retention checklist

- [ ] Send a check-in 7 days after delivery.
- [ ] Ask one question: "Anything you'd change?"
- [ ] Note the answer. Use it next time.
- [ ] Offer the next logical step (retainer, upsell, referral).
- [ ] Add them to a quarterly touch list.

## The rule

Silence is not satisfaction. Check in before they forget you exist.
