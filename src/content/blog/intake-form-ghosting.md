Ghosting isn't random. It's what happens when a client never felt the stakes.

## The 5-question intake

1. What's the one outcome you want in 30 days?
2. What's the budget ceiling — be honest, I'll tell you if it's enough.
3. What's the deadline that actually matters?
4. Who else needs to approve this?
5. What's the one thing that would make you cancel?
