# Pricing Your Time Without Guessing

This is the actual worksheet. Not a theory. The math.

## Step 1: Your real hourly rate

Take your annual income goal. Divide by 50 weeks. Divide by the hours you actually work (not 40 — the real number).

Example: $80,000 / 50 / 35 = $45.71/hour.

That's your floor. Never go below it.

## Step 2: The project multiplier

For fixed-price work, multiply your hourly rate by estimated hours, then add 30% for the shit you didn't see coming.

Example: 10 hours × $45.71 = $457. Add 30% = $594.

## Step 3: The value check

Ask: what is this worth to them? If your number is lower than theirs, raise it.

## The rule

If you can't explain your price in one sentence, it's too complicated. Simplify.
