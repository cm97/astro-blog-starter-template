## The checklist

**Do it the same time every week. Friday afternoon or Monday morning. Pick one and keep it.**

1. **Inbox to zero action items.** Not zero unread — zero things waiting on a decision from you. Reply, delegate, snooze, or kill each one.
2. **Scan for overdue follow-ups.** Anyone you said you'd get back to and didn't? Surface them now, before they become a lost client.
3. **Top 3 priorities for next week.** Not 10. Three keeps you honest.
4. **Look one week back.** What got done? What slid twice? A task that slides twice usually belongs on someone else's list — or in the trash.
5. **Schedule the first task.** Put the #1 priority on the calendar with a time. Intentions without a slot die.

That's it. Twenty minutes. The reason most people drop it isn't the time — it's that there's no system reminding them to sit down and do it. That's the part the Digital System automates for you.
