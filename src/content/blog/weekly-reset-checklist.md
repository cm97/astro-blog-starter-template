Most solo operators don't fail because they lack ideas. They fail because the week eats the plan by Wednesday and by Thursday everything is back in their head.

This is the exact reset I run. Steal it. Print it. Tape it to the monitor.

## The 20-minute reset

1. **Clear the inbox to zero.** Archive or reply. No "I'll get to it later" pile.
2. **List the three outcomes that matter this week.** Not tasks. Outcomes. "Onboard two clients" beats "send emails."
3. **Block 90 minutes for deep work.** Calendar it. Defend it like a client meeting.
4. **Schedule the follow-ups.** Every open conversation gets a date on the calendar, not a mental note.
5. **Kill one thing.** One commitment, one tab, one project that isn't moving. Delete it.
6. **Write the next week's first task.** So Monday morning isn't a blank page.

That's it. Twenty minutes. Do it Friday afternoon or Monday morning — whichever you won't skip.
