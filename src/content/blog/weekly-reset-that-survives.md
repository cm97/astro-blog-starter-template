Monday you plan. Thursday you forget. By Friday you're firefighting and the plan is a joke.

This is the reset that stops that.

## The 20-minute reset

1. **Clear the inbox to zero.** Archive or delete. Don't organize — delete.
2. **Pick the one thing that moves revenue this week.** Not ten things. One.
3. **Schedule it.** Put it on the calendar like a client meeting. Non-negotiable.
4. **Kill two tasks.** If they're still there next week, they were never important.
5. **Write the next follow-up date.** Every open lead gets a date or it dies.

## Why it survives

Because it's short enough to actually do, and the Digital System has it as a printable card you can stick on your monitor. No app. No 47 tabs. Just the card.
