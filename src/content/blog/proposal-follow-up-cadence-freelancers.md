A proposal with no reply is not a maybe. It is a job you have not closed or cleared. Send three dated notes. Then stop.

## Day 3 — one ask

Subject: Proposal — still the right fit?

Hi [Name], checking the proposal I sent on [date]. One question: do you want to start, or should I close the file? Reply with start or close.

## Day 7 — date and next step

Subject: Closing this on [date] unless I hear back

Hi [Name], I have not heard back on the [project] proposal. I will close it on [date, 5 days out] so the slot does not sit open. If you want to start, reply with the start date and I will send the welcome note the same day.

## Day 12 — clear the pipeline

Subject: Closed the proposal

Hi [Name], I closed the proposal today. If the timing changes, reply to this email and I will reopen it. No chase after this.
