Monday starts and the open proposals, unpaid deposits, and quiet clients all live in different tabs. By 11am one of them has already aged past the window where a short email still works.

You do not need a new CRM. You need one 15-minute scan that surfaces the three people who need a message today so the rest of the week does not depend on you remembering them.

## The 15-minute Monday scan

Open a blank note or the Today screen. Write these three rows. Cap each at three names. More than that is noise.

```
Open proposals past day 5: ________  ________  ________
Unpaid deposits past day 2: ________  ________  ________
Quiet clients (no reply 7+ days mid-project): ________  ________  ________
```

Fill only the ones that are actually due. If a row is empty, write none. Do not invent names to feel busy.

## Exact next action for each row

**Open proposals past day 5**

Send the binary:

> Still want me to hold the [start date] for [project], or should I release it?

Subject: Re: [Project name]

One sentence. Mid-morning before 11am. Log the send time. Do not check for 24 hours.

**Unpaid deposits past day 2**

Send:

> Invoice for the [project] deposit is in your inbox. Still good to lock the [start date], or should I release it?

Same window. Same log. Do not start work.

**Quiet clients 7+ days mid-project**

Send:

> Quick check on [project]. Still on track for [next milestone date], or do you need me to hold?

One line. Before 11am. Update the next touch date the second it leaves.

## The log that survives Thursday

After each send, fill this in the same note:

```
Name: ________
Row: proposal / deposit / quiet
Touch sent: ________ (date/time)
Next check: ________
Status: waiting / replied / closed
```

Three minutes total. The scan is useless if the next date is blank.
