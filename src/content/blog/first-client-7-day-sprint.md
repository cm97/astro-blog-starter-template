You have no clients. You have no audience. You have a skill and a laptop. You think you need to build a brand first. You do not. You need one paying client. Here is how to get one in seven days.

## Day 1: Pick your niche

Not "I do everything." One thing. One type of client. One problem you solve. "I build landing pages for coaches" beats "I do web design" every time.

## Day 2: Find 20 prospects

Not 200. Twenty. People who have the problem you solve. LinkedIn, Instagram, local business directories. Write down their names.

## Day 3: Send 20 messages

Not a pitch. A question. "I noticed you are struggling with X. I have a fix. Want to see it?" Short. Specific. No attachment.

## Day 4: Follow up

Half of them will not reply. That is normal. Follow up once. "Bumping this — still interested?" Then move on.

## Day 5: Offer a free audit

To the ones who replied: "I will audit your current setup for free. Takes 15 minutes. You get a report. I get to show you what I can do." This is not charity. It is a sales call disguised as help.

## Day 6: Deliver the audit

Do it well. Be specific. Show them exactly what is broken and exactly how you would fix it. Then: "I can do this for you. Here is the price. Here is when we start."

## Day 7: Close or move on

If they say yes, send the invoice. If they say no, thank them and move to the next prospect. Do not beg. Do not discount. Move on.

## Why this works

It is a system, not a hope. You are not waiting for clients to find you. You are hunting them down with a repeatable process.
