You do not need a brand, a following, or a funnel to get paid. You need one person with the problem, one short message, and a way to start the work the same day they say yes. If the first week after they pay is rebuilt from memory, they go quiet and you are back at zero.

This is the sprint. Run it this week. Do not add a logo first.

## Day 1 — one offer, one buyer

Write one sentence: who you help, the broken thing, the result, the price.

Fill this in and stop editing it:

- I help: [one type of person, not "everyone"]
- Who is stuck on: [one visible problem]
- So they can: [one outcome in a week]
- Price to start: [one number you will say out loud]

"I build a one-page offer for coaches who are still explaining their service in DMs" beats "I do marketing."

## Day 2 — 20 names, not 200

Open the app you already use. Write 20 names of people who already show the problem. LinkedIn, Instagram, a local directory, old texts. No cold list you bought.

For each name, one line: what is visibly broken. A dead link, a vague bio, a proposal they posted and never followed up, a "DM me for prices" with no prices.

## Day 3 — 20 messages, one question

Not a pitch deck. Copy this and swap the bracket:

> Saw [specific thing]. Most [role] lose the next client there because [one reason]. I have a 15-minute fix. Want it?

No attachment. No "just circling back" on day one. Send all 20 in one sitting.

## Day 4 — one follow-up, then stop

Half will not reply. That is the job, not a rejection. One bump on day 4:

> Bumping this — still useful, or should I close it?

Then move. Do not write a new novel.

## Day 5 — the 15-minute audit, not free work

To anyone who replied:

> I will look at [the broken thing] for 15 minutes and send three fixes. You keep the notes. If you want me to do the fixes, I will quote the number from day 1.

Cap it. A free afternoon is not a sales call.

## Day 6 — deliver the audit, name the price

Three bullets only:

1. What is broken
2. What you would change this week
3. Price and start date

Then: "I can do this. Here is the price. We start [day]."

## Day 7 — invoice or next name

Yes means invoice the same day. No means thank them and go to the next name. Do not discount to "get a win." A cheap first client who needs a custom process is a second unpaid job.

## The hour after they pay

This is where most first clients die. Do these five things before you celebrate:

1. Send the welcome email with the start date, what you need from them, and when you will reply.
2. Send the intake: goal, deadline, access, who decides, what is out of scope.
3. Book the kickoff. Agenda is already written: outcome, constraints, first deliverable, next check-in.
4. Put the 48-hour follow-up on the calendar so it does not depend on memory.
5. Log them in a one-page client note: name, paid, next step, date.
