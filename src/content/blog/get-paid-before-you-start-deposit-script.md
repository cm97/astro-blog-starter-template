## Why starting before money lands loses the job

A verbal yes is not payment. Work that starts before the deposit clears becomes free work the moment they go quiet. You cannot un-send the first draft.

The sequence that keeps the client is one clear ask, one amount, one due date, and no work until the money clears.

## The 8-minute close the minute they say yes

Do these in order. Do not open the project file until step 6 is done.

1. **Confirm the scope in one sentence.** Reply: "Great. Scope is [deliverable] for [price]. I start once the 50% deposit clears." Time box: 1 minute.
2. **Send the deposit request the same hour.** Not tomorrow. Not after you "get organized." The ask is part of the close, not a separate task.
3. **Name the amount and the due date.** 50% of the total, due in 48 hours. Write the number before you write the email.
4. **Give one payment path.** Stripe link or the invoice link you already use. Do not offer three options.
5. **State the rule.** No work starts until the deposit clears. One sentence. No apology.
6. **Log it.** Same place you log weekly work. Date requested, amount, due date, status.
7. **Set the day-2 bump.** Calendar hold for 48 hours later. If it has not cleared, send the bump. Do not start the work.
8. **Start only after it clears.** Then run the onboarding checklist. Not before.

Fill this before you send the deposit email:

```
Client: ________

```

## The script

When they say yes:

> Confirmed. Scope is [deliverable] for [total price]. I start once the 50% deposit of [amount] clears. Pay here: [link]. Due by [date, 48 hours]. No work starts before that.

No "looking forward to it." No "let me know if you have questions." One confirmation, one amount, one link, one rule.

If they ask "can we start now and pay later?":

> I can start the day the deposit clears. That keeps the schedule you named. Pay the [amount] by [date] and we open the file.

If they go quiet after the request:

> Checking the deposit on [project]. Still want the [start date]? Reply and I will hold it, or I release the slot.

Day-2 bump, same thread:

> Deposit of [amount] still open. If it clears by [tomorrow], we keep the original start. Otherwise I release the date.

## Exact timing that gets the money

- Send the deposit request the same hour they say yes. Not the next morning.
- Due date is 48 hours. Not "when you can."
- Bump at hour 48 if it has not cleared. One line. Then release the date if still quiet by hour 72.
- Never start the file on a verbal yes. The calendar hold is for the bump, not for the work.
