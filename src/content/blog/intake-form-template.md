# Intake Form Template

This is the actual form. Copy it into Google Forms or Typeform.

## The questions

1. What's the main thing you need help with? (One sentence.)
2. What's your deadline?
3. What's your budget range?
4. Have you worked with someone like me before?
5. What does success look like to you?
6. Is there anything I should know before we talk?

## The rule

If they won't fill it out, they weren't serious. Send it before the call, not after.
