You are between calls or in the car. The proposal went quiet three days ago. The new client has not sent the intake. You know a follow-up should go out.

It does not, because the list lives in a desktop note and the right email is buried in a doc you cannot open from your phone without rewriting it.

By Thursday the window is gone and the deal dies in your head.

The fix is a system that works from the phone you already have. No new apps. No desk required.

## The 8-minute phone scan

Do this every Monday morning, or the first quiet 8 minutes you get. Same order. Timer on. Stop at 8.

1. Open the one note (or the Today screen) that holds open proposals, missing intakes, and quiet clients. Cap each list at three names. More is noise.
2. For every proposal older than 5 days with no reply, copy the day-5/7 line and send it.
3. For every new client whose intake is still blank after the deposit, send the intake nudge.
4. For every active client quiet more than 7 days, send the mid-project check-in.
5. Write the next check date next to each name before you close the app. Blank next-date means the scan failed.

That is the entire process. Four actions. Eight minutes. The emails are already written so you paste, fill the blank, send.

Fill this log on the phone before you start:

```
Date: ________
Proposals >5 days, no reply: ________ ________ ________
Intakes missing: ________
Quiet mid-project (>7 days): ________ ________
Next scan date: ________
```

If any field is blank after the 8 minutes, the scan was incomplete. Do not open new work until the four actions are done.

## Exact lines you paste from the phone

Copy these. Fill the blanks. Send. Do not rewrite while you are standing in line.

## Timing rules that keep replies coming from the phone

- Send all follow-ups before 11am, Tuesday–Thursday only.
- Never Friday. The thread dies over the weekend.
- After you send, write the next check date in the log the same minute. The phone note is the system.
- Cap the scan at three names per category. More than that is noise; the rest wait until next Monday.
- If a reply lands mid-week, update the log in 60 seconds. Do not rewrite the email.

## Thursday cash salvage from the phone (6 minutes)

When Monday never happened, do this instead of opening the full list.

1. Open the note. Name the one late delivery or the one quiet proposal that still has a date.
2. Send the exact proposal or mid-project line above. One only.
3. Write the next date. Close the app.

That keeps cash moving without a desk.
