## The scenario

New client, "Sarah," signs on Monday. Budget is tight, deadline is Friday. You have 15 minutes before your next call.

## What you do (the checklist, filled in)

**Before she signs**
- Scope: "Website copy refresh, 3 pages, no SEO, done by Friday."
- Intake form sent: name, goal, deadline, budget, the one thing she keeps forgetting.
- Welcome message: "Hey Sarah, here's what happens next. Reply to this when you've filled the form."

**Day one**
- Form comes back. You confirm receipt and the start date.
- You drop her into the follow-up sequence (one calendar reminder for Thursday).

**First week**
- 15-minute kickoff. Agenda sent ahead: "3 things to cover, 12 minutes."
- First small win: you send a draft of page 1 by Wednesday. She feels momentum.
- You log what worked: "Intake form saved 20 minutes."

## Why this matters

Without the checklist, Monday looks like: panic, rebuild from memory, forget the follow-up, lose the client by Thursday. With it, Monday looks like: open file, check boxes, done.
