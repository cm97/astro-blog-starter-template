Onboarding is where most one-person businesses leak money. Not because the work is hard — because every new client means rebuilding the same steps from memory, and memory is a terrible project manager.

## The onboarding checklist

1. **Welcome message** — sent within 1 hour of payment. Sets expectations and the next step.
2. **Intake form** — name, goals, deadlines, access needed. One form, not five emails.
3. **Kickoff call** — 20 minutes. Confirm scope, timeline, and what "done" looks like.
4. **Access & assets** — collect logins, brand files, and references in one place.
5. **First deliverable plan** — a short written plan the client can see and approve.
6. **Check-in cadence** — when you'll update them and how (email, call, Loom).
7. **Handoff** — final files, invoice, and a "what's next" note.
