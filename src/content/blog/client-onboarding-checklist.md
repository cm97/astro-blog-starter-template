Onboarding is where most one-person businesses quietly bleed. Not because they don't care — because the process lives in their head, and heads get full.

This is the checklist I actually run. Steal it.

## Before the client signs

- [ ] One-page scope: what you're doing, what you're not, and what "done" looks like.
- [ ] A simple intake form (name, goal, deadline, budget, the one thing they keep forgetting to mention).
- [ ] A welcome message template that sets expectations in two sentences.

## Day one

- [ ] Send the intake form and the welcome message.
- [ ] Confirm receipt and the start date.
- [ ] Drop them into your follow-up sequence (even if it's just a calendar reminder).

## First week

- [ ] Kickoff call or message — 15 minutes, agenda sent ahead.
- [ ] Deliver the first small win so they feel momentum.
- [ ] Log what worked and what didn't, so next time it's faster.

## The trap

Most people skip the logging step. Then the next client starts from zero again. The Digital System has this checklist pre-built with the logging step baked in, so you never rebuild it.
