Pricing by gut feeling is how you end up broke and busy. Here's a floor, not a ceiling.

## The quick method

1. **Hourly floor** — what you need per hour to survive. Multiply by estimated hours.
2. **Value price** — what the outcome is worth to them. Charge a slice of that.
3. **Pick the higher one.** Don't apologize for it.
4. **Add a buffer** — 20% for the stuff you always forget.

## The rule

If a client balks at the number, the scope was wrong, not the price. Adjust the scope, not the rate.
