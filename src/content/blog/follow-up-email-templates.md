Most follow-up emails are either too long or too desperate. These are neither.

## Template 1 — The value drop

"Saw this and thought of you. [One useful link.] No ask."

## Template 2 — The soft nudge

"Checking in on [project]. Anything I can unblock for you this week?"

## Template 3 — The close

"Ready to move forward, or should I close this out? Either way, no hard feelings."

## The rule

Every email ends with a question that has a yes/no answer. "Let me know your thoughts" is not a question. The Digital System ships all three as editable templates with subject lines that don't sound like spam.
