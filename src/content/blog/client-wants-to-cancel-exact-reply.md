## Why a soft yes loses the retainer

"Totally understand, take your time" removes the friction. They never come back. Or they come back at a lower rate because the original number was never restated.

The reply that keeps the client is two options only: keep the current cadence at the current price, or lock a specific restart date with a smaller scope. No third option of "I'll just pause indefinitely and hope."

## The 5-minute reply the hour the note arrives

Do these in order. Do not agree to an open-ended pause in the first message.

1. **Pause in one line.** Reply: "Got it. Let me check the current cadence so I can give you two clean options." Time box: 1 minute.
2. **Open the last status email or onboarding note.** Write down the current monthly price, the last deliverable, and the next scheduled touch. If those three lines do not exist, that is the bug.
3. **Name what continues and what stops.** One sentence each. Clients forget they are still getting the Friday update.
4. **Send two options only.** Keep the retainer as-is, or reduce to a lighter monthly check-in with a restart date.
5. **Ask for a written choice by a date.** "Reply with the option and the date and I lock it."
6. **Log it on the client list.** Same place as the weekly reset. Price, options sent, status.
7. **Set the day-3 bump.** If they go quiet, one line. Do not invent a discount to restart the thread.

```
Client: ________

Status: waiting / chose A / chose B / ended
```

## The script

When they ask to pause or cancel:

> I can keep the current retainer at $[price] — Friday status, next deliverable on [date], same scope. Or we drop to a lighter monthly check-in at $[lower] and I restart the full work on [specific date]. Which do you want?

No apology. No "I hate to lose you." One restatement, two options, one question.

If they say money is tight:

> Understood. Full retainer stays $[price]. Lighter option is $[lower] with one status and one deliverable per month, restarting full on [date]. Reply with the one that fits and I lock the next invoice.

If they want to end completely:

> We can close the file. Last invoice covers through [date]. After that I will send one check-in on [date 30 days out] in case you want to restart. Reply confirm and I mark it closed.

If they go quiet after the two options:

> Checking which option you want for the retainer. Full at $[price] or lighter at $[lower] restarting [date]. Reply by [day] or I will treat it as ended on the current cycle.

## Exact timing that protects the number

- Send the two-option reply the same day. Not after you sleep on it.
- Do not drop the price in the first reply even if they sound stressed.
- Bump at 72 hours if no choice. One line. Then close the file if still quiet by day 7.
- Never invent a "keep half price forever" option. It trains the next ask.
