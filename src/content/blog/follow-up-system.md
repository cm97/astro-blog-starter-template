Follow-ups are where money goes to die. Not dramatically — quietly. A lead goes cold. A client drifts. You meant to get back to them. You didn't. They bought from someone who did.

Here's the system I use. It's boring on purpose.

## The cadence

- **Day 0:** Reply within 24 hours. Even if it's just "got it, here's what happens next."
- **Day 3:** One short check-in. No pitch. Just "anything I can help with?"
- **Day 7:** A value drop — a tip, a resource, a relevant post. Not a sales email.
- **Day 14:** A direct ask. "Ready to move forward, or should I close this out?"
- **Day 30:** Archive or re-engage. No limbo.

## The rules

1. Every follow-up has a date on it. If it doesn't have a date, it doesn't exist.
2. Every follow-up has a next step. "Just checking in" is not a next step.
3. Every follow-up is logged somewhere that isn't your brain.

## Why this works

It removes the decision. You don't decide whether to follow up — the cadence decides for you. The Digital System has this cadence as a ready-to-run checklist, so you never have to remember the days.
