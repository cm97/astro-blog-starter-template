Follow-ups die in the inbox because there's no system holding them. You send an email, you feel good, and then life happens and you never circle back.

## The follow-up checklist

- [ ] Log the follow-up the moment you send it (client, date, what you asked).
- [ ] Set a reminder for 3 business days later.
- [ ] On the reminder day, send one short line: "Checking in — any questions?"
- [ ] If no reply in 2 more days, send the final nudge or close the loop.
- [ ] Mark it done. Don't let it linger.

## The rule

A follow-up without a reminder is a wish. Put it on the checklist, not in your head.
