Follow-ups die because they're vague. "Just checking in" is not a follow-up. It's a shrug.

## Three templates that work

**1. The soft nudge (3 days after no reply)**
> Hi [Name] — wanted to make sure this didn't get buried. Happy to adjust the plan if timing changed. Want me to hold the slot or close it out?

**2. The value drop (7 days)**
> Hi [Name] — sent over a quick resource that might help with [specific thing]. No pressure to reply, but if you want to pick this back up I'm around.

**3. The break-up (14 days)**
> Hi [Name] — I'll assume the timing isn't right and close this out on my end. If that changes, just reply to this email and we'll pick it up.
