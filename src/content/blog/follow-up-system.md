Follow-ups are where money goes to die. Not because you don't want to follow up — because "I'll get back to them" is not a system. It's a hope.

Here's the cadence I use. It takes minutes a week and it stops the slow leak of leads you never see leave.

## The three buckets

1. **New leads** — anyone who reached out in the last 7 days and hasn't heard back. Reply within 24 hours, even if it's just "got it, here's what happens next."
2. **Warm leads** — interested but not decided. One check-in at day 3, one at day 7. After that, one final nudge, then let it go.
3. **Clients** — anyone mid-project. A weekly status, even if it's "nothing to report, still on track." Silence reads as abandonment.

## The rules that keep it alive

- **Write it down the moment you say it.** "I'll send that Thursday" becomes a dated entry, not a mental note.
- **Batch it.** 15 minutes, twice a week, dedicated. Not "when I have a second" — you never have a second.
- **Close or kill.** Every open thread gets a reply, a snooze, or a delete. Open threads are the ones that rot.

## What this is not

This is not a CRM with 40 fields. It's three buckets and a habit. The Digital System has the actual checklists and the weekly reset that keeps the buckets from overflowing — see below.
