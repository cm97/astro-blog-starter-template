Follow-ups die because they're not scheduled. You mean to send them. You don't. This fixes it.

## The 3-touch follow-up

1. **Day 1** — Send the deliverable + one question.
2. **Day 3** — Check in. "Any questions?" Short.
3. **Day 7** — Close the loop. Summary + next step or goodbye.

Put it on a calendar. Same time every week. It takes 5 minutes.
