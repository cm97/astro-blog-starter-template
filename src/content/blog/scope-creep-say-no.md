Scope creep doesn't arrive as a monster. It arrives as "can you just quickly..."

## The script

"Happy to do that. It's outside the original scope, so it's a separate $X or we swap it for something else. Which do you prefer?"

## The rule

Never say no to the request. Say yes to the request and no to the free part. The Digital System has a scope-change log template so every "quick add" gets a price tag and a signature.
