You hit send on the proposal. Forty-eight hours later the thread is still silent. You keep checking. You rewrite the follow-up in your head. Nothing leaves your phone.

That silence is where most closes die. Not the price. Not the scope. The missing 48-hour poke.

Send this exact email. Same words every time. No blank screen. Calendar it the moment the proposal leaves so the 48-hour mark cannot invent a new version.

## What you log the minute the proposal goes out

Open the same note you already use for Monday. Fill this block in under three minutes.

```
Name: ________
Proposal sent: ________ (date and time)
One-line scope: ________
Price: ________
Start date offered: ________
48-hour due: ________
Status: waiting / replied / closed
Next action if no reply: send 48-hour / park / close
```

Put a 4-minute block on the calendar labeled "48h: [name]". If it lands after 3pm or on Friday, move it to the next weekday morning. Do not skip.

## The 48-hour email

Send it before 11am on the due day. Not when you feel ready.

> Subject: Re: [proposal name]
>
> Quick check — did the proposal land, or should I resend the link?
>
> I am holding [start date] until [date + 5 days].
>
> One line is enough if you want me to keep the slot.

Fill-in before you send: only name a start date you actually offered. Do not add a paragraph about how busy you are. Do not attach a new PDF. Time box: four minutes including finding the thread.

Phone-paste version (under 160 chars):

> Quick check — proposal land, or resend? Holding [start date] until [date]. One line enough.

## What you do if they answer

- **Yes / start:** send the deposit request the same hour. Welcome note with the start date, the one thing you need from them, and the day they will hear from you next.
- **Not now:** write the month they named on the log. One note that month. Not a weekly drip.
- **Price question:** restate what is in and what is out. Do not cut the number to get a reply.
- **Scope change:** new line, new price, new date. The original proposal stays the record.
- **No / close:** mark closed. Do not argue.

If you cannot name the next date after a yes, you do not have a running client yet. You have a payment waiting on a process that still lives in your head.

## Extra concrete steps that stop the leak

1. Block the 48-hour send the day the proposal leaves. Unscheduled follow-ups die.
2. Copy the log into the same note as your Monday reset. One place.
3. If they ask a question, answer in one paragraph and restate the hold date. Do not open a new scope conversation.
4. After the 48-hour, do not send a second version because you felt guilty on a quiet Friday.
5. At the next weekly reset, add a row: "Proposals past 48 hours." If none, write none.
6. Cap open proposal rows at five. Anything past five days is closed or parked before you write the three weekly priorities.
7. Never send after 3pm. Move those windows to the next morning.
8. Screenshot the log or copy it into the same note as the weekly reset the same hour you send the proposal. Blank next action means the thread dies again.
