You open your laptop at 9am. By 9:15 you have checked email, replied to three messages, and forgotten what you were supposed to do today. By noon you have done three hours of work that was not the work that pays.

This is not a discipline problem. It is a structure problem. You have no blocks. Everything is one big blur of "stuff to do."

## The 3-block system

**Block 1: Deep work (9am–12pm).** The thing that makes money. Writing, building, designing. No email. No Slack. No "quick questions." Phone in another room.

**Block 2: Admin and communication (1pm–3pm).** Email, client messages, invoicing, scheduling. Batch it. Do it all at once. Do not let it leak into Block 1.

**Block 3: Planning and review (3pm–4pm).** What got done. What did not. What is next. Update the weekly reset checklist. Close the laptop.

## Why this works

It gives your day a spine. You are not reacting to whatever pings first. You are executing a plan. The deep work block is sacred — it is the only block that directly generates revenue.
