A proposal doesn't need to be 10 pages. It needs to be clear.

## The 1-page proposal

1. **The goal** — one sentence, in their words.
2. **The approach** — 3–5 steps, no jargon.
3. **The deliverables** — exactly what they get.
4. **The timeline** — dates, not "ASAP."
5. **The price** — one number, one payment option.
6. **The next step** — "Reply yes and I'll send the invoice."

## The rule

If they can't say yes to one sentence, the proposal is too long.
