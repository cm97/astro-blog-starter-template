Long proposals lose. Short ones that name the problem, the plan, and the price win.

## The 4-block proposal

1. **The problem** — in their words, not yours.
2. **The plan** — 3 steps max. What you'll do, in order.
3. **The price** — one number, one payment option.
4. **The next step** — "Reply yes and I'll send the kickoff link."

That's the whole thing. The Digital System includes this as an editable template so you stop writing proposals from scratch every time.
