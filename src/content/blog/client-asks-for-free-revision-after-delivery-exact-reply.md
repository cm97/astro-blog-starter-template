You delivered the final. The client replied two days later with “can you just tweak this one more thing?” The project is marked done. The invoice is paid or sitting open. One free revision turns into three and the next client starts later.

A free revision after delivery is scope that already closed. The reply has to name the close, name the price for the new work, and leave the original file finished.

## The 3-minute log before you reply

Open the same note you use for the Monday scan. Fill this block.

```
Name: ________
Original delivery date: ________
Original scope line: ________
Requested change: ________
Is it inside original scope: yes / no
Price for the change if no: $________
Status after reply: closed / new quote / paid
```

If the original scope line is blank, do not reply yet. Paste the line from the proposal first.

## Exact reply — send today

Subject: Re: [Project name] — final delivery

> Hi [Name],
>
> The final delivery on [date] covered [one-line original scope].
>
> The change you described is outside that scope.
>
> I can do it as a separate piece for [price] and deliver by [date]. Reply yes and I send the short quote.
>
> The original file stays closed.
>
> [Your name]

One paragraph. No apology. No “let me know if that works.” The original stays closed. The new work has a price and a date.

Send it before 11am on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday. Never Friday. Never after 3pm.

Phone-paste version (under 160 chars):

> Final on [date] covered the original scope. Change is extra at [price], deliver by [date]. Reply yes for the quote. Original stays closed.

## What you do the minute it leaves

1. Mark the original file closed in the log.
2. Write the new quote line if they say yes: same note, new row.
3. Do not open the original file again until the new quote is paid.
4. If they say yes, send the short quote the same hour. Do not wait until tomorrow.
5. If they go quiet, leave the original closed. Do not follow up on the free ask.

## Extra concrete steps that stop the free revision from opening

- Copy the original scope line into the reply before you hit send. Memory versions get softer.
- Cap free revisions at zero after the final delivery date written in the proposal. Anything after is a new line item.
- If they paid the final invoice already, the reply still names the new price. Paid does not reopen the file.
- Put a 3-minute calendar block titled “Scope close: [Name]” the night before if you already know the ask is coming.
- After you send, screenshot or copy the sent time into the log. “I think I replied” leaves the door open.
- Never offer a “quick fix” in the same thread. The quick fix becomes the free revision.
- If they reply “it was implied,” paste the original scope line again and the price. Do not debate.
- On the next weekly reset, move every closed delivery older than 7 days out of the active list. The list is for cash still open.
- Pre-write the subject and body in the calendar note the day the final delivery goes out. The 3-minute block only needs a paste and send.
- Keep the five most common extra-ask replies in the same note so you never search under pressure.
- If they ask for a call about the free revision, answer: “Happy to talk after the short quote is yes or no. Original file stays closed either way.”
- At Friday close, count how many free-revision asks you closed that week. Write the number on the Monday still-open line. Zero open free asks is the goal.

## Exact replies for the answers you actually get

When they reply, do not invent a new email. Paste one of these.

**Can you just do it quickly?**
> The original scope is closed. The change is [price], deliver by [date]. Reply yes for the quote.

**It was part of the original.**
> The delivery on [date] covered [exact scope line]. This sits outside it. Separate quote is [price].

**Can we hop on a call?**
> Happy to after a yes or no on the [price] quote. Original file stays closed either way.

**I already paid.**
> Payment covers the original scope delivered on [date]. The new change is still [price]. Reply yes for the quote.

**Just this once.**
> Scope stays closed. The price for this change is [price], deliver by [date]. Reply yes if you want it.

**They pay the new quote:**
Open a fresh row in the log. Do not reopen the old file. Send the short confirmation the same hour.

## Fill-in after every reply

```
Reply sent: ________ (date/time)
Original file closed: yes / no
New quote sent: yes / no / pending
Reply within 48h: yes / no
If yes: paid / still quiet / debate
Next action: new work started / closed for good
```

## Why this works when “I’ll just do it this time” fails

“I’ll just do it this time” trains the client that final delivery is soft. The exact reply names the close and the new price. The clients who want the work pay. The ones who do not free the calendar for the next paid client.
