Monday opens. Three proposals are still quiet. One said they would decide last week. One never answered the first follow-up. One is a referral who liked the call and then vanished.

You open the inbox, stare, and close it. The hour is gone. The leads stay quiet.

Run the scan instead. Same order every Monday. Cap it at 20 minutes. Then the emails leave.

## The 20-minute scan

Do these steps in order. Do not open a new document.

1. List the three quiet leads on one note. Name, last contact date, what you sent, cash still open. Time box: 3 minutes.
2. Rank them by cash at risk. Largest open fee first. Time box: 1 minute.
3. For each, pick the next line from the set below. Do not invent a new paragraph. Time box: 4 minutes.
4. Fill the two blanks in each email (name + project or date). Time box: 3 minutes.
5. Send all three. Do not wait for a better time. Time box: 4 minutes.
6. Log the send date and next touch on the same note. Time box: 2 minutes.
7. Close the note. The scan is finished. Anything left waits until Wednesday or next Monday.

Fill this before you start sending:

```
Quiet leads this Monday:
1) ________ — last contact ________ — open fee ________ — next line: ________
2) ________ — last contact ________ — open fee ________ — next line: ________
3) ________ — last contact ________ — open fee ________ — next line: ________
Sends today (max 3): ________
Next scan if still quiet: ________
```

## The lines you paste

Use only these. Swap the blanks. Do not add a fourth paragraph.

**Day-3 after first proposal (they have not replied at all):**

Subject: Quick check on [project]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Checking that the proposal for [project] landed. Reply with any question or a yes and we lock the start date.
>
> [Your name]

**Day-7 decision line:**

Subject: Still holding the slot for [project]?

> Hi [Name],
>
> Still holding the start date we discussed for [project], or should I release the slot?
>
> One line is enough.
>
> [Your name]

**After they said “let me think”:**

Subject: Decision on [project]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Checking back on the decision for [project]. Reply with yes, no, or a new date and I will lock it.
>
> [Your name]

**Referral who went quiet after the call:**

Subject: Next step on [project]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up from our call on [project]. Reply with the one thing still blocking a yes and I will answer it in one line.
>
> [Your name]

Send the matching line. Log it. Move to the next lead.

## Extra rules that keep the scan under 20 minutes

- Cap at three sends. A fourth lead waits until the next scan.
- If a lead has already received the day-7 line, move it to paused. Do not write a novel.
- Write the open fee next to the name before you rank. Cash decides the order, not how much you like them.
- Send before you open any delivery file. The scan dies once client work starts.
- If the day-3 lands on a delivery day, still send it in the first 90 seconds of the morning.
- After you send, do not check for a reply the same hour. The next touch is already on the note.
- If two leads need the same line, paste it twice. Do not customize the middle paragraph.
- On the next Monday, open the same note first. Update the dates. Do not start a new list.
