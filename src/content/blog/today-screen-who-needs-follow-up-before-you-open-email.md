## The 10-minute Today scan

Open a blank note or the Today screen. Write only these three rows. Cap each at three names.

```
Proposals with no reply past day 3: ________  ________  ________
Invoices unpaid past day 1: ________  ________  ________
New clients with onboarding steps due today: ________  ________  ________
```

Fill only the ones that are actually due. If a row is empty, write none. Time box the scan at 10 minutes.

## Exact next action for each row

**Proposals with no reply past day 3**

Send the short nudge:

> Hi [Name], just checking in on the proposal I sent for [project]. Still interested or should I close the file?

Subject: Re: [Project name]

One sentence. Mid-morning. Do not add a second paragraph. Log the send and move on.

**Invoices unpaid past day 1**

Send:

> Invoice [number] for [project] is in your inbox. Still good to lock the start date or should I release it?

Same window. Do not start work.

**New clients with onboarding steps due**

Send the next step only:

> Welcome aboard. Here is the intake form so we can lock the kickoff for [date].

One message. Then mark the step done.

## The log that survives Thursday

After each send, write:

```
Name: ________
Row: proposal / invoice / onboarding
Touch sent: ________
Next check: ________
Open cash: $________
```

Three minutes. The scan is useless if the next date is blank.
