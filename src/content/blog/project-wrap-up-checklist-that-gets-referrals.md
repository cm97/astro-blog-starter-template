The project ends. You send the final file. Then silence. No thank-you, no testimonial, no referral, and six weeks later you are starting from zero with a new lead while the old client has forgotten your name.

That is the failure mode. The finish lives in your head. The last email is delayed. The ask never happens. The next opportunity dies because you closed the laptop.

Run this checklist the day the last deliverable ships. Fifteen minutes. Same steps every time. If you only have eight, stop after the final email and the testimonial line.

## The 15-minute wrap-up checklist

1. **Send the final delivery email (4 minutes).** Subject: Final [deliverable] — [project name]. Attach or link the work. One sentence on what is included. One sentence on what is not. Date the email so the record exists.
2. **Ask for the testimonial in the same thread (3 minutes).** Three lines: specific result they can quote, the exact ask, and the reply window. Do not wait for a perfect moment.
3. **Log the private close note (3 minutes).** Client name, end date, result achieved, any open item, and the next touch date (30 or 60 days). If it is not written, the client is gone.
4. **Set the 30-day check-in (3 minutes).** Put it on the same list as your weekly reset. Title: [Client] 30-day. The hold exists before you move on.
5. **Send the one-line next-step offer (2 minutes).** Optional but high leverage: “If you want help with [next related thing], here is the one-page outline. Reply if useful.” Only if it fits. Do not invent work.

That is the sequence. Copy the emails once. Reuse them.

Fill this before you leave the laptop:

```
Client: ________
Project end: ________ (date)
Final delivery sent: yes / no — time: ________
Testimonial asked: yes / no
Private close note written: yes / no
30-day check-in on calendar: ________
Next-step offer sent: yes / no / not applicable
```

## Exact final delivery email

> **Subject:** Final [deliverable] — [project name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Attached is the final [deliverable]. It covers [exact scope line from the agreement]. Anything beyond that was quoted separately.
>
> Thank you for the clear brief and the fast feedback. The work is done on my side.
>
> [Your name]

Send it the same day the file is ready. Do not let it sit overnight.

## Exact testimonial ask (same thread, same day)

> Hi [Name] — glad this landed. If the [specific result] was useful, would you reply with two or three sentences I can quote? Even a short note helps the next person decide. No pressure if now is not the time.

Keep it under four lines. The ask is specific. The out is clear. Most people who will say yes reply within 48 hours.

## Private close note

```
Client: ________
Ended: ________
Result: ________
Testimonial received: yes / no
Open risk or leftover: ________
30-day touch: ________
Referral potential: high / medium / low
Amount paid: $________
```

Write it next to the weekly reset. If the note is missing, the client is not closed.

## 30-day check-in email (pre-write it now)

> Hi [Name] — quick check on the [project]. How is [the result] holding up? Anything new on your side that needs a look?

Put the block on the calendar the same afternoon you send the final delivery. The email is one line. The calendar is the reminder.
