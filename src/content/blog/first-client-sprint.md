You don't need a perfect offer. You need a week of focused action.

## The 7-day sprint

- **Day 1:** Pick one service, one price, one sentence that describes it.
- **Day 2:** List 20 people who might need it. No overthinking.
- **Day 3:** Send 10 personalized messages. Not a blast. Personal.
- **Day 4:** Follow up the 10 who didn't reply.
- **Day 5:** Do a free 15-minute call with anyone who responds.
- **Day 6:** Send a one-page proposal to the best fit.
- **Day 7:** Close or move on. Don't linger.
