It's Friday afternoon. Your phone has three open proposal threads and one deposit invoice that said yes on Tuesday. None of them moved. By Monday the threads will be buried and you will rewrite the follow-ups from scratch.

That weekend gap is where most closes die. Not the price. Not the offer. The missing five-minute decision force before the week ends.

Run this exact scan and send these lines. Same words every Friday. No blank screen. Calendar the block so it happens even when delivery is on fire.

## The 2-minute log you open at 3pm Friday

Open the same note as your Monday reset. Add this block.

```
Friday scan date: ________
Open proposals past 48h: ________ (list names)
Unpaid deposits past day 2: ________ (list names)
Decision forced today: yes / no for each
Next Monday action: ________
Status after send: waiting / closed / paid
```

Two minutes. If the list is empty, write none and close the app. Empty is a win.

## The 5-minute Friday scan order

1. Open the note. List every proposal sent more than 48 hours ago that has no reply.
2. List every deposit invoice sent more than 48 hours ago that is unpaid.
3. For each, decide: send the close line, or park it with a named date.
4. Send the emails before 4pm. Never after.
5. Update the log. Set the Monday reminder.
6. Close the note. Weekend is free of "I should follow up."

Cap the list at five total. Older than five days gets closed or parked before you write the lines.

## Exact Friday email for a quiet proposal

Send before 4pm. Same subject every time.

> Subject: Re: [proposal name]
>
> Quick close on the [proposal] — still good to lock [start date], or should I release the slot?
>
> One line is enough.

Fill only the start date you already offered. Do not add a paragraph. Do not attach anything new.

Phone-paste version:

> Quick close — still good for [start date] or release the slot? One line enough.

## Exact Friday email for an unpaid deposit

> Subject: Re: [project] deposit
>
> Holding [start date] until end of day. Reply if you still want it, or I release the slot.
>
> One word works.

Same rules. Binary. No story.

## What you do when they reply over the weekend or Monday

- Yes / send it: request the deposit the same hour if it is a proposal. If deposit, mark paid and send the welcome note with the one intake item you need.
- Not now / later: write the exact month they named. One note that month. Status parked.
- Processing: restate the hold until the clearance date they give. Log it.
- No / never mind: mark closed. Do not argue.
- Silence by Monday morning: treat as closed. Do not send a second version because you felt guilty.

If you cannot name the next date after a yes, you do not have a running client. You have a payment waiting on a process that still lives in your head.

## Extra concrete steps that stop the Friday leak

1. Block the 5-minute scan on the calendar for 3pm every Friday. Unscheduled dies.
2. Copy the log into the same note as the Monday reset. One place.
3. Never send after 4pm Friday. Move the whole block to Thursday if you know you will be offline.
4. After any yes, the deposit or welcome note goes out the same hour. Delay and the close cools.
5. At the next weekly reset, add a row: "Friday closes that landed." If none, write none.
6. Cap open rows at five. Anything past five days is closed or parked before the scan.
7. Screenshot the log or copy it into the same note the same hour you send. Blank next action means the thread dies again.
8. If they reply "still thinking," mark a new 5-day hold and put the next Friday scan on the calendar. Do not invent a third version.
9. Keep the exact subject lines in the log so the next client does not require a rewrite.
10. Never start work on a "they said they would pay Friday" without the money in the account.

## Phone-only version (from your phone, under 5 minutes)

When you are not at a desk.

1. Open the note with the Friday log. List the names.
2. Paste the short version into email or Messages for each.
3. Log the send time next to the name.
4. Set a phone reminder for Monday morning: check replies.
5. Close the app. Done.

Exact short reply if they answer with a question:

> Scope stays [one line]. Price stays [number]. Start still [date] if you reply by Monday.
