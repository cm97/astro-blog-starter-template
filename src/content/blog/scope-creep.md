Scope creep is how good clients become bad ones. Not because they mean harm — because you never defined the edges.

## The no-script

> "I can do that. It sits outside what we agreed, so it's a separate $X or we swap it for something else in the current scope. Which do you prefer?"

That's it. Calm, specific, gives them a choice. No apology, no over-explaining.
