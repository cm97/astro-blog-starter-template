Scope creep is how good clients become bad weeks. They ask for "just one more thing" and suddenly you're working for free.

## The script

- "Happy to do that — it's outside the original scope, so it'd be a separate $X. Want me to send a quick quote?"
- "I can fit that in next week. This week's focus stays on [the agreed deliverable]."
- "Love the idea. Let's park it and revisit after we ship this."

## The rule

Say no to the work, not to the person. The relationship survives. The free labor doesn't.
