This is the single habit that's done the most for how I run Buzzyfly, and it costs about 20 minutes. No app required, though the Digital System has a version of this built in with the checklist ready to go.

**Do it the same time every week — Friday afternoon or Monday morning both work, pick whichever matches how your brain resets.**

1. **Clear the inbox to zero *action items*.** Not zero unread — zero things sitting there waiting on a decision from you. Reply, delegate, snooze, or kill each one.
2. **Scan for follow-ups that are quietly overdue.** Anyone you said you'd get back to and didn't? This is where they surface before they turn into a lost client.
3. **Pick your top 3 priorities for next week — not 10.** If everything's a priority, nothing is. Three keeps you honest.
4. **Look one week back.** What actually got done? What kept getting pushed? A pattern of the same task sliding twice usually means it doesn't belong on your list — it belongs on someone else's, or in the trash.

That's it. It's not clever. It's just consistent, and consistency is the entire game. The reason most people don't keep it up isn't the 20 minutes — it's that there's no system reminding them to sit down and do it. That's the part worth automating.
