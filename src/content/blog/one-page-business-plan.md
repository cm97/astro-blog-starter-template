Business plans are usually 40 pages of fiction. This is one page of reality.

## The page

**Top of page:** Your one-sentence goal for the next 90 days. Not a vision. A goal. "Get 5 new clients" or "Launch the new offer."

**Middle:** Your three numbers — revenue target, cost floor, and the one metric that tells you if you're on track.

**Bottom:** Your next three moves. Concrete. Dated. Not "explore options."

## Why one page

Because you'll actually look at it. A 40-page plan goes in a drawer. A one-page plan goes on your wall. The Digital System has this as a printable template, plus a weekly version that fits on a sticky note.
