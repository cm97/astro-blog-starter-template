You paid for the system. Now don't let it sit in a forgotten browser tab like every other tool you signed up for in a fit of optimism.

## What's in the app

Open your access link and you'll see six tabs:

- **Today** — who needs a follow-up, which onboarding steps are due, and this week's #1.
- **Clients** — your leads, proposals, active clients and open invoices.
- **Emails** — follow-ups, onboarding, proposals and scope replies, filled in with each client's details.
- **Reset** — the 20-minute Monday checklist and the priority scorer.
- **Pricing** — your hourly floor, project and retainer prices.
- **More** — your sign-off, backups and help.

No 200-tab spreadsheet. No course with 40 videos you'll never watch. Just the screens you need this week.

## The 5-minute setup

1. **Save your access link.** Bookmark it, or add it to your phone's home screen. Out of sight is out of system.
2. **Add your sign-off.** Every email ends with your name.
3. **Add the clients you're working with right now.** Pick the stage each one is at and the date it started. The app works out the follow-ups.
4. **Open Today and send what's due.** Copy the email, change anything in [brackets], and send it from your own inbox.

## When it breaks

It will. You'll forget a step, a client will throw a curveball, the week will eat you. That's fine — the system is the floor, not the ceiling. The point isn't perfection. It's that the process still exists on the weeks you are slammed.
