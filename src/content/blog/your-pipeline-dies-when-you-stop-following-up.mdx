You had the call. It went well. You sent the proposal the same day. You felt the deal was yours.

Then nothing.

Day three: you open the email draft and close it. "They will reply."

Day seven: you tell yourself you will follow up after this other deadline.

Day fourteen: the lead is dead and you never even got a no. You just stopped.

This is not a prospecting problem. It is a system problem. You have no follow-up process that survives a busy week. So the pipeline quietly bleeds out while you are busy delivering for the clients who already said yes.

## The real cost

Every silent proposal is unpaid work. The discovery call, the research, the custom pricing — all of it evaporates because there was no sequence running after the send button.

Most freelancers treat follow-up as a feeling: "I should check in." Feelings lose to deadlines. Systems do not.

## The three-step sequence that keeps deals alive

**Day 3 — The light bump**  
One short email. No new pitch. Just: still interested? Any questions? Done.

**Day 7 — The value add**  
Send something useful related to their problem. A link, a one-line insight, a relevant example. Remind them you exist without asking for the sale again.

**Day 14 — The clean close**  
Assume it is not the right time. Leave the door open. People reply to break-ups far more than they reply to endless nudges.

Run it the same way every time. No deciding. No waiting for motivation.
