You onboard client one from memory. It works, barely. Client two, you forget a step. Client three, you send the wrong file. By client five you're rebuilding the whole thing from scratch every time.

That's not a you problem. That's a no-system problem.

## The fix

Write the steps once. Put them in a checklist. Run the checklist every time. When something changes, update the checklist — not your memory.

The Digital System is that checklist, plus the weekly reset and follow-up templates, so the process survives the weeks you're slammed.
