You do the weekly reset on Monday. You feel organized. By Thursday you have forgotten it exists. The checklist is buried under three new projects. The plan is a joke. You are back to reacting.

The problem is not the reset. It is the reset. You built a system that requires you to remember to run it. That is not a system. That is a wish.

## The reset that survives

**Monday (15 min):** Review last week. What got done. What did not. Write the three priorities for this week. Not ten. Three.

**Wednesday (5 min):** Mid-week check. Are the three priorities still the priorities? If not, adjust. Do not add. Adjust.

**Friday (10 min):** Close the week. What got done. What rolls over. Update the checklist. Close the laptop.

## Why this works

It is short. It is scheduled. It does not require you to remember. It is a habit, not a hope.
