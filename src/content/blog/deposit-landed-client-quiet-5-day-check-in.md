Deposit hit. You sent the welcome note. Then silence. No intake. No files. No reply to the kickoff date you offered. The project is not moving and you are already rewriting the follow-up in your head.

That quiet window is where most new clients stall. Not because they changed their mind. Because no one sent the next exact message on day five.

Send this check-in. Same words every time. Calendar it the moment the deposit clears so day five cannot invent a softer version.

## What you log the hour the deposit lands

Open the same note as your Monday reset. Fill this in under three minutes.

```
Name: ________
Deposit cleared: ________ (date)
Welcome sent: ________
Kickoff date offered: ________
5-day due: ________
Status: waiting / replied / started
Next action if quiet: send 5-day / nudge intake / start without
Files expected: ________
Scope line locked: yes / no
```

Put a 4-minute block on the calendar labeled "5d: [name]". If it falls after 3pm or on Friday, move it to the next weekday morning. Do not skip.

## Day-by-day so the stall cannot hide

**Day 0 (deposit clears)**
- Fill the log above.
- Send welcome + intake in the same hour.
- Block the day-5 send.
- Add one line to next Monday’s reset: "[Name] — 5-day check due [date]."

**Day 1–2**
- Do not send anything new. The welcome is still warm.
- If they reply with files, confirm the kickoff in one sentence and stop.

**Day 5 (the check-in)**
- Send the exact note below before 11am.
- Log the send time the same minute.

**Day 7 if still quiet**
- One intake nudge only: "Still need the form and files to keep [kickoff date]. Link: [link]. Deadline is [date + 2]."
- Do not rewrite the 5-day note.

## The 5-day check-in

Send it before 11am on the due day.

> Subject: Re: [project name] — next step
>
> Quick check on the intake and kickoff for [project].
>
> I am holding [kickoff date] if the form and files are back by [date + 2 days].
>
> One line is enough if you want me to keep the slot or move it.

Fill-in before you send: only name a date you actually offered. Do not add a paragraph about how busy you are. Do not attach a new PDF. Time box: four minutes including finding the thread.

Phone-paste version (under 160 chars):

> Quick check — intake and kickoff for [project]. Holding [date] if form and files by [date]. One line enough.

## What you do if they answer

- **Yes / sending files:** confirm the kickoff date in one sentence and stop. Do not open new scope.
- **Need more time:** name the new date they gave. Log it. One follow-up only.
- **Question on scope:** restate what is in and out. Do not cut price.
- **No / pause:** mark paused. Do not chase.

If you cannot name the next date after a reply, the process still lives in your head.

## Extra concrete steps that stop the stall

1. Block the 5-day send the hour the deposit clears. Unscheduled check-ins die.
2. Copy the log into the same note as your Monday reset. One place.
3. If they ask a question, answer in one paragraph and restate the hold date. Do not open a new conversation.
4. After the 5-day, do not send a second version because you felt guilty on a quiet Friday.
5. At the next weekly reset, add a row: "Deposits past 5 days quiet." If none, write none.
6. Cap open deposit rows at three. Anything past five days is started or parked before you write the three weekly priorities.
7. Never send after 3pm. Move those windows to the next morning.
8. Screenshot the log or copy it into the same note as the weekly reset the same hour the deposit lands. Blank next action means the thread dies again.
9. If three deposits land the same week, run only Day 0 and the 5-day for each. Park extra intake reviews until the Monday reset.
10. Phone-only: dictate the 5-day note into Notes, paste, send, mark the calendar block done. The log still has to exist in the same note as the reset.
