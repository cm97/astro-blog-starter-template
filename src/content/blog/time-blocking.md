Time blocking fails when it's too rigid. Life happens. Here's a version that bends.

## The 3-block day

- **Block 1 (90 min):** Deep work. The thing that moves the needle. No email, no Slack.
- **Block 2 (60 min):** Admin and client comms. Batched, not scattered.
- **Block 3 (30 min):** Planning tomorrow. Write it down, close the laptop.

That's three hours of focused time. The rest is buffer. The Digital System includes a weekly time-block template so you can set this up in ten minutes.
