# Time Blocking for Solo Operators

This is the actual system. Not a philosophy. The blocks.

## The blocks

- **9-11am:** Deep work. No email. No meetings.
- **11am-12pm:** Email and admin.
- **12-1pm:** Lunch. Actually eat.
- **1-3pm:** Client work or calls.
- **3-5pm:** Content, planning, or whatever's left.

## The rule

If your day has no blocks, it has no structure. Block it or lose it.
