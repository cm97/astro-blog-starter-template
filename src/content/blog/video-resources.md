You don't need a camera, a mic, or editing skills. You need a script and one free tool.

## The script (already written)

It's in [What You Get: The Explainer](/blog/explainer-video-script/). Copy it. Paste it into the tool. Done.

## The tools (all free to start)

- **Synthesia** — AI avatar talks your script. Free tier: 3 minutes/month. Good enough for one explainer.
- **Pexo** — paste the script or a URL, get an explainer. Free credits to start.
- **CapCut** — stock footage + your voiceover. Free, no watermark on most exports.
- **Canva** — simple motion graphics if you want slides instead of a talking head.

## The 4-step workflow

1. Open the script post.
2. Pick one tool. Synthesia is fastest if you hate your own voice.
3. Export 1080p, under 90 seconds.
4. Upload to R2 or YouTube (unlisted) and link it from the store hero.
