Monday is not the problem. The first hour is.

You sit down already late. Three messages. One fire. No written plan. So you rebuild the week from memory, again.

Do this instead. Twenty minutes. Same order every time.

## 1. Clear the inbound pile, do not work it

Open email and DMs. Sort only: reply today, wait, delete. Do not write the replies yet.

## 2. Name three priorities, not twelve

Write one client delivery, one follow-up that is aging, and one admin item that unblocks money. Everything else waits until those three move.

## 3. Touch every open client once

One line each: next deliverable and next date. If you cannot name the next date, that client is drifting.

## 4. Send the two follow-ups you have been avoiding

Proposal with no reply. Finished project with no check-in. Use a template. Do not draft from a blank screen.

## 5. Stop.

If you keep going you will spend the morning organizing instead of shipping.
