The names live in your head. Heads do not hold dates when Slack is pinging and a client is waiting on the draft. ## The 20-minute Today process

Do this every morning (or the first 20 minutes of the day). Same order. Timer on. Stop at 20.

1. Open the list of who is due today. If the list is empty, write "none" and move to delivery.
2. Send the exact follow-up or onboarding step that is already written for each name.
3. Log the send and set the next date the same minute.
4. One unpaid invoice older than 1 day gets the reminder.
5. Close the screen. Do not reopen email until the 20 minutes are done.

That is the entire process. Five actions max. The emails are already filled with the name.

## Extra concrete rules that keep the list alive

- Open the Today list before you open email or Slack. If it is empty, write "none" and leave.
- Cap the morning at three sends. A fourth means you are rewriting the day. Park the rest until tomorrow.
- Paste the next date into the note the same minute you send. Future-you will not remember the math.
- If they reply mid-morning, update the date in 60 seconds. Do not rewrite the email.
- Move any Slack or text ask into the same note within 15 minutes. The channel that created the noise is not the channel that records the next date.
- At the end of the 20 minutes, screenshot or copy the three sent items into the weekly reset note. The morning scan disappears if it only lives in sent mail.
- Never discount in the follow-up. The original number stays the record.
- After any yes or deposit, open the onboarding sequence the same hour. The follow-up ends when money lands.
- If the list shows more than five names, close or park anything older than 21 days. Old silence is not a warm lead.
- Set the next check as a calendar hold titled "[Client] next touch" so tomorrow's list finds it without searching.
- If you only have time for one email, send the oldest due item. The rest wait until the next 20 minutes.
- Log every send with the exact subject you used. Tomorrow's list will not re-send the same one by accident.
- Write the amount still open next to the name the day you add them. Blank fields mean the follow-up has nothing to point at.
- If a due date falls on a weekend, move it to Friday morning. Do not slide it to Monday and lose the window.
- Keep the five templates in the same screen as the list. One open, all available.
