Calendars feel like cages. This method feels like freedom.

## The three blocks

1. **Deep work block (2 hours):** One hard task. No email, no Slack, no "quick questions."
2. **Admin block (1 hour):** Email, invoices, scheduling. Batch it.
3. **Buffer block (30 min):** The stuff that always spills over. Protect it.

## Why it works

Because you're not scheduling every minute. You're protecting three chunks. The Digital System has this as a weekly time-block template you can print and stick on your wall.
