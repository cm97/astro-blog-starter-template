# Lead Magnet Ideas

This is the actual list. Not theory. What works.

## What works

- A checklist they can print and use today
- A template they can copy and send
- A one-page guide that solves one problem
- A calculator or worksheet
- A short video walkthrough

## What doesn't work

- A 50-page ebook nobody reads
- A webinar nobody attends
- A "free consultation" that's really a sales pitch

## The rule

Give them something they can use in 5 minutes. If it takes longer, it's not a lead magnet.
