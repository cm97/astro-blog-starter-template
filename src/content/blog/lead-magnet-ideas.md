Most lead magnets fail because they are long, vague, or require a login. Solo operators already have zero extra time. They will not download a 40-page ebook.

Give them one thing they can open and use in five minutes. Then sell the system that keeps the habit.

## What works (paste-ready)

- **20-minute weekly reset checklist.** One page. Clear the board, three priorities, two follow-ups, tomorrow's first move. They run it Monday and feel the difference before they ever buy.
- **Day-3 proposal follow-up line.** One sentence they can send today: "Still want me to hold the [date] start, or should I release it?"
- **New-client first-10-minutes sequence.** Welcome email + five intake fields + calendar hold. Copy, swap the name, send.
- **Unpaid-invoice day-3 note.** "Invoice for [deliverable] is attached. Due [date]. Reply if the address is wrong."
- **Scope-creep one-liner.** "Happy to do that — it's $X and adds two days. Want me to add it or keep the original scope?"

These are the exact assets people request after they hit a slammed week. The free weekly-reset version is already on the homepage. The rest live inside the Digital System.

## What does not work

- 50-page ebooks
- Webinars that require a calendar slot
- "Free strategy call" that is just a sales pitch
- Anything that needs an account or a login before they see value

If they cannot use it in the same sitting they download it, it is not a lead magnet. It is homework.
