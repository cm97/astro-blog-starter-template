Ghosting usually starts before the work does. A client reaches out, you reply, they vanish. The fix is an intake form that makes them invest a little before you do.

## The 5-question intake

1. What's the goal in one sentence?
2. What's the deadline?
3. What's the budget range?
4. What does "done" look like?
5. How do you want to communicate?

## The rule

If they won't fill it out, they weren't serious. Send the form before the call, not after.
