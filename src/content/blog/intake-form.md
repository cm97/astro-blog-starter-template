Ghosting usually isn't rudeness. It's friction. Too many emails, too many questions, no clear next step.

## The 5-field intake

1. Name + best way to reach you
2. What you want done (one sentence)
3. Deadline or "no rush"
4. Budget range (optional, but filters tire-kickers)
5. Anything I should know before we start

Send it once. If they don't fill it, they weren't ready. The Digital System includes a ready-to-copy form plus the email that sends it.
