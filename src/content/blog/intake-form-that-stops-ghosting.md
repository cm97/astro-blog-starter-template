You get a lead. You send a long email explaining everything. They never reply. You send another. Nothing. You assume they are busy. They are not busy. They are not interested. And you wasted an hour writing an email they never read.

The fix is not a better email. It is a form. A short form that forces them to invest two minutes before you invest an hour.

## The intake form

**1. What is the one thing you need fixed?**
One sentence. If they cannot answer this, they do not have a problem. They have a vibe.

**2. What have you tried so far?**
Tells you if they are a tire-kicker or a real prospect. Tire-kickers have tried nothing. Real prospects have tried three things and failed.

**3. What is your budget range?**
Ask it straight. Range implies flexibility. The second phrasing implies a ceiling.

**4. When do you need this done?**
Urgency filter. If the answer is "whenever," they are not serious. If the answer is "by Friday," they are.

**5. Who else is involved in the decision?**
If it is not just them, you are not selling to them. You are selling to a committee.

## Why this works

It filters. The people who fill it out are invested. The people who skip it were never going to buy. You save your time for the ones who matter.
