# Discovery Questions

This is the actual list. Ask these before you pitch.

## The questions

1. What's the main problem you're trying to solve?
2. What have you tried already?
3. What does success look like in 90 days?
4. What's your budget?
5. Who else is involved in the decision?
6. What's your timeline?
7. Is there anything that would make you walk away?

## The rule

If they can't answer question 1, they're not ready. Walk away.
