Bad discovery questions get bad answers. "What are your goals?" gets "grow the business." Useless.

## Ten questions that work

1. What's broken right now that you want fixed?
2. What have you already tried?
3. What does success look like in 30 days?
4. What's the budget ceiling?
5. Who else is involved in the decision?
6. What's the deadline that can't move?
7. What would make you say "this was worth it"?
8. What would make you say "waste of money"?
9. Is there a constraint I should know about (time, access, people)?
10. If we do nothing, what happens?
