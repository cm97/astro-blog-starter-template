Run this checklist the day payment clears. Twenty minutes. Same steps every time.

## The 20-minute onboarding checklist

1. **Send the welcome email (4 minutes).** Subject: Welcome — [project name]. Three lines: payment received, what happens next, the one thing you need from them today. Link or attach the intake.
2. **Send the intake form (3 minutes).** Five questions max. Name, most important outcome, what they already tried, who else decides, anything else. Deadline: 3 days.
3. **Book the kickoff (4 minutes).** 30–45 minutes in the next three business days. Title: [Client] kickoff. Invite them now. The hold exists before you close the laptop.
4. **Write the private note (4 minutes).** Client name, start date, first-week goal from the intake or payment note, next check-in date. If it is not written, it dies.
5. **Set the day-7 check-in (5 minutes).** Put it on the same list as your weekly reset. Not “sometime.” A date.

That is the whole sequence. No new tools. Copy the welcome and the intake once. Reuse them.

## Exact welcome email

> **Subject:** Welcome — [project name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Payment received. Welcome.
>
> Next step is the short intake form (link). Once that is back I will send the kickoff agenda and the first-week checklist.
>
> Kickoff is on the calendar for [day/time]. Reply if that slot does not work.
>
> [Your name]

Send it before you do anything else.

## Exact intake fields

- Most important outcome from this engagement?
- What have you already tried that did not stick?
- Who else needs to be in the loop on decisions?
- What would make this feel like a clear win in 90 days?
- Anything else I should know before kickoff?

Five fields. Phone-friendly. No account required.
