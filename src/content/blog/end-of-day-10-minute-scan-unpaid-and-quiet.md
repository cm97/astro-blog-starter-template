## The 10-minute end-of-day scan

Open the same note you already use for Monday. Fill only these rows. Time box: 10 minutes. Stop at the timer.

1. **Unpaid invoices past 5 days** — list name, amount, days overdue, last message date. If none, write none.
2. **Proposals silent past 48 hours** — list name, price, date sent, next action (48h sent / park / close).
3. **Clients quiet mid-project past 5 days** — list name, last contact, one-line status, next nudge date.
4. **Deposits received but intake still missing** — list name, deposit date, days waiting. If none, write none.
5. **One action for tomorrow morning** — the single send or call that clears the highest-risk row. Write the exact subject line now.

Copy the finished block into tomorrow's first calendar slot labeled "scan follow-up". If the slot lands after 3pm, move it to the next morning. Do not leave the rows in your head.

## Exact lines you paste

**Unpaid invoice day 7:**

> Subject: Invoice [number] — quick check
>
> Checking in on invoice [number] for [amount]. Sent [date]. Happy to resend or answer anything that is blocking payment.

**Quiet proposal day 3:**

> Subject: Re: [proposal]
>
> Quick check — did the proposal land, or should I resend? Holding [start date] until [date + 3].

**Quiet mid-project:**

> Subject: Quick status on [project]
>
> Wanted to confirm we are still on track for [next milestone]. Anything you need from me before [date]?

Fill only the blanks. Do not add a paragraph about how busy you are. Do not attach a new file. Four minutes per send including finding the thread.

## What you do with the answers

- **Payment lands:** mark paid the same hour. Send the next onboarding step if it is a new client. Do not open a new conversation.
- **Proposal yes:** deposit request the same day. Welcome note with the one thing you need and the day they will hear from you next.
- **Still silent:** park the row for the next Monday scan. Do not send a second version because you felt guilty at 9pm.
- **Scope question:** restate what is in and what is out. New price only if the scope changes. Original proposal stays the record.

If you cannot name the next date after a yes, you do not have a running client yet. You have a payment waiting on a process that still lives in your head.

## Extra concrete steps that stop the leak

1. Block the 10-minute scan at 4:50pm every weekday. Unscheduled scans die.
2. Keep the scan in the same note as the Monday reset. One place.
3. Cap open unpaid and quiet rows at six. Anything past that is closed or parked before you write tomorrow's one action.
4. Never send after 6pm. Move those windows to the next morning.
5. Screenshot the scan or copy it into the same note the same hour you finish. Blank next action means the thread dies again.
6. At the next weekly reset, add a row: "End-of-day scans completed." If you skipped, write skipped and the reason in one line.
7. If a client replies after hours, answer in one paragraph the next morning and restate the hold date. Do not open a new scope conversation at night.
