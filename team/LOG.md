  Community post 1 (r/freelance or a freelancer Facebook group), title "What I send when a client sits on files and the deadline slides": Most stalled projects I've seen aren't ghosting. What's worked for me is one email in the same thread that does three things. 1) List exactly what's missing as bullets ("final logo as SVG", "your notes on round 1"), not "feedback". 2) Move the date in writing: "since these were due on [date], delivery moves from [old] to [new].
