## What it is and what it pays (checked 2026-11-02)

- AppSumo is a marketplace for software deals aimed at entrepreneurs (it says
  it reaches 1.5 million). Listing has no upfront cost.
- **Revenue share:** about 95% to us for buyers we send there, and about 30% to
  us (70% to AppSumo) for buyers AppSumo brings. Featured deals have their own
  negotiated terms.
- **Obligations:** keep the listing up for at least 120 days. AppSumo's own
  refund window for its customers (about 60 days) applies to those sales. A
  lifetime deal means updates and support for the life of the product.
- **Owner decisions before applying:**
  1. Accept AppSumo's refund window for AppSumo sales. It's longer than our
     30-day rule, which still applies on buzzyfly.com.
  2. Choose the deal price. Suggested: $49, the same as the site, so there's
     no price conflict.
  3. Choose a single-user license (recommended) or a team license.

## Listing draft (real features only, no invented numbers)

**Product name:** Buzzyfly Digital System

**Tagline (under 60 characters):** Know who to follow up with today, and what to say

**Short description:**
A browser app for freelancers, coaches and small agencies. Add your leads,
proposals and clients. Buzzyfly shows who needs a follow-up today and writes
the email with their name, project and dates filled in. New clients get the same
onboarding steps every time, and a 20-minute Monday reset keeps the week on
track. Works on phone and computer. No spreadsheets, nothing to install.

**Key features:**
- Today list: due follow-ups, overdue invoices and onboarding steps, by date
- Email writer: 21 built-in emails (proposal follow-ups, invoice reminders,
  welcome, intake, kickoff, scope and discount replies) plus your own, filled in
  for each client
- Copy, or open straight in your email app. Mark it sent, and the next follow-up is scheduled
- Onboarding steps for every new client
- Monday reset (20 minutes) with live counts and a priority scorer
- Pricing calculator: hourly floor, project and retainer prices
- Backup and restore. Data stays in the buyer's own browser

**Who it's for:** freelancers, coaches, consultants and small agencies who lose
work to forgotten follow-ups and messy onboarding.

**Who it's not for:** teams that need a shared CRM with many users, and anyone
already running a documented system that works.

**Honest limits (put these in the listing):**
- Data is saved in the buyer's browser on one device. Use backup and restore to move it.
- No team accounts, no email sending from the app (it opens your email app), and no integrations yet.
