# Scope Creep Checklist

## When they ask for more
- [ ] Don't say "that's not in scope"
- [ ] Don't say "I can't do that"
- [ ] Say: "That's a great idea. It's outside this phase, so here's what I'd suggest: [option 1] or [option 2]. Which feels better?"
- [ ] Give them two options, both of which respect your time
- [ ] Make them choose

## The rule
If you can't say this out loud without flinching, practice it in the mirror.
