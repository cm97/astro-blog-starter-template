# Client Onboarding Checklist
### Run this every time. Same order. Every client.

---

## PHASE 1 — Before the kickoff call (complete within 24 hours of payment)

- [ ] Send the welcome email (`templates/welcome-email.md`) within 1 hour of payment received
- [ ] Send the intake form link — do NOT book the call until it's filled out
- [ ] Send the contract if not already signed
- [ ] Create the client folder: `[ClientName] — [StartDate]`
  - Subfolder: `/communications` (every email thread summary goes here)
  - Subfolder: `/deliverables` (every file you send them)
  - Subfolder: `/notes` (call notes, observations, flags)
- [ ] Add client to your calendar: kickoff call + 4 weekly check-ins already blocked
- [ ] Read the intake form before the kickoff call — highlight anything that concerns you
- [ ] Prepare 3 questions you couldn't answer from the intake form
- [ ] Confirm payment received and logged

**Red flags to watch before the call:**
- Intake form left blank or answered with one word
- "ASAP" in the deadline field with no real date
- Budget listed as "negotiable" or left blank
- "I've tried other [service providers] and it didn't work" without explanation

---

## PHASE 2 — The kickoff call

- [ ] Start on time. End on time. Respect sets the tone.
- [ ] Confirm their #1 goal in their words, not yours — write it down
- [ ] Confirm what "done" looks like (specific, measurable)
- [ ] Confirm timeline: milestones with actual dates, not "around week 3"
- [ ] Confirm deliverables: list them out loud, have them agree
- [ ] Confirm communication channel (email / Slack / text) and response window
- [ ] Confirm who on their side has final approval
- [ ] Ask: "Is there anything I should know that wasn't in the intake form?"
- [ ] Tell them your office hours and when they can expect replies
- [ ] End the call by confirming the next step and who does it

**Do NOT leave the call without:**
- A confirmed first milestone date
- A confirmed communication channel
- A clear answer to "what does success look like"

- [ ] Send the written call summary within 24 hours (use `templates/kickoff-summary.md`)

---

## PHASE 3 — After the kickoff call (within 48 hours)

- [ ] Summary email sent and acknowledged
- [ ] Project created in your tracking system (folder, board, or doc — whatever you use)
- [ ] First milestone date added to your calendar with a 3-day warning reminder
- [ ] Any assets or info you need from them — request in one email, not ten
- [ ] Add to your weekly retention list (`checklists/retention.md`)
- [ ] Note the date 2 weeks from delivery to send the follow-up check-in

---

## PHASE 4 — First week touchpoints

- [ ] Day 3: one-line email confirming you've started (removes anxiety on their end)
- [ ] Day 7: send the first small deliverable or progress update — even if it's just a status
- [ ] Day 10: check if they've sent everything you requested; send a polite nudge if not

---

## The standard

If you handed this checklist to someone else and they could run the onboarding without asking you a single question, it's done right.

If that's not true yet, you have a gap. Find it and fix it.
