# Intake Form Checklist

## Before the call
- [ ] Send the intake form link
- [ ] Wait for it to be filled out — don't schedule until it is
- [ ] Read it before the call so you're not wasting their time

## The questions
1. What's the main thing you need help with? (One sentence.)
2. What's your deadline?
3. What's your budget range?
4. Have you worked with someone like me before?
5. What does success look like to you?
6. Is there anything I should know before we talk?

## The rule
If they won't fill it out, they weren't serious.
