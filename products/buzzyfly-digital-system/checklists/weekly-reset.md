# The 20-Minute Weekly Reset
### Run this Monday morning (or Sunday night). No longer than 20 minutes.

---

## Before you start

Set a timer for 20 minutes. When it goes off, you stop — even if you're not done. The discipline of stopping is the point. If it regularly takes longer than 20 minutes, something is wrong with your system, not your willpower.

Open last week's reset notes before you write anything new.

---

## PART 1 — Last week review (5 minutes)

- [ ] What were last week's 3 priorities?
- [ ] What actually got done? (Be honest. Not what you started — what you finished.)
- [ ] What didn't get done? Write one reason why. Not an excuse — a real reason.
- [ ] Did any client go more than 5 days without contact? (Name them.)
- [ ] Did you say yes to something you should have said no to? (Note it.)
- [ ] One thing you did well last week: ______________________
- [ ] One thing to do differently this week: ______________________

---

## PART 2 — This week's priorities (5 minutes)

Write exactly 3 things. Not 5. Not 10. Three.

These are the things that, if nothing else gets done, the week still counted.

| Priority | Why it matters | Due |
|---|---|---|
| 1. | | |
| 2. | | |
| 3. | | |

**For each priority, ask:**
- Does it move money or a client relationship forward?
- Is it actually mine to do, or should I delegate or drop it?
- Is the deadline real or invented?

---

## PART 3 — Calendar and inbox sweep (5 minutes)

- [ ] Look at every meeting this week — delete or decline anything that isn't necessary
- [ ] Block 2 uninterrupted hours for deep work (not 9–10am on a Monday — pick a real slot)
- [ ] Anything in your inbox older than 7 days: reply, archive, or delete. No exceptions.
- [ ] Any proposal out longer than 5 days with no response: trigger follow-up (`checklists/follow-up.md`)
- [ ] Any client expecting a deliverable this week: confirm it's on track

---

## PART 4 — Admin sweep (5 minutes)

- [ ] Any unpaid invoices older than 7 days? Send a polite reminder today.
- [ ] Any contracts waiting to be signed? Follow up.
- [ ] Any tools or subscriptions you're paying for but not using? Flag for cancellation.
- [ ] Weekly revenue this week vs last week: up / flat / down (circle one)
- [ ] If down — what's one thing you can do this week to change it?

---

## Midweek check-in (Wednesday — 5 minutes max)

Run this Wednesday afternoon, not morning.

- [ ] Are the 3 priorities still on track? If no — cut one, not two. Pick the one that matters most.
- [ ] Any client follow-up rotting in your inbox? Send it today.
- [ ] Any scope creep conversations you've been avoiding? Have them today.
- [ ] Are you on pace for your weekly revenue number?

---

## End-of-week close (Friday — 5 minutes)

- [ ] What got done? Write it down. You will forget by Monday and it matters.
- [ ] What didn't get done? Move it to next week's list or cut it.
- [ ] Send any Friday check-ins to active clients
- [ ] Clear your desktop and close your tabs
- [ ] Close the laptop. Actually close it.

---

## Quarterly reset (run every 13 weeks)

- [ ] How many clients did you onboard? Close? Lose?
- [ ] What was your average project value?
- [ ] What was your actual hourly rate (total revenue ÷ total hours worked)?
- [ ] What did you spend the most time on that wasn't billable?
- [ ] What's the one system change that would have the biggest impact next quarter?

---

## The rule

If the weekly reset takes more than 20 minutes, you're running a strategy session, not a reset. Stop. The reset is maintenance, not planning. Keep it tight.
