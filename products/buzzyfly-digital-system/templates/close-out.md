# Close-Out Email Templates
### For leads who go quiet, projects that wrap, and relationships you're ending cleanly.

---

## TEMPLATE 1 — The breakup email (unresponsive lead, day 14–18)

**Subject: Should I close this out?**

Hi [Name],

I've reached out a couple of times and haven't heard back, so I'm going to assume the timing's off.

I'll close this thread on my end. If things change and you want to pick it up later, you know where to find me.

No hard feelings either way.

[Your name]

---

**Why this works:** It creates a soft urgency without pressure. The phrase "close this on my end" triggers the instinct to respond. You'll get replies from leads you'd written off — sometimes immediately, sometimes months later. Even if you don't, you've gotten clarity and can move on.

## TEMPLATE 2 — Project close-out (at end of engagement)

**Subject: [Project Name] — wrapped up**

Hi [Name],

Everything for [project name] is finalized and delivered. Here's a quick summary of what's included:

- [Final deliverable 1]
- [Final deliverable 2]
- [Final deliverable 3]

All files are at [location — shared folder, email attachment, etc.].

**A few notes:**
- [Anything they should know going forward — maintenance, passwords, instructions]
- If you run into any issues in the next [X days], reach out and I'll help sort it out

It's been genuinely great working on this. [Specific thing you appreciated — not generic flattery, something real.]

I'll check in in a couple of weeks to see how things are landing.

[Your name]

## TEMPLATE 3 — Mutual "not a fit" close (early in the process)

**Subject: Good talking — wanted to be straight with you**

Hi [Name],

It was good to connect. After thinking it over, I don't think I'm the right fit for what you're describing right now.

[Optional: one brief honest reason — "I don't have bandwidth to give this the attention it deserves right now" or "This is a bit outside my core focus area."]

I'd rather be upfront than waste your time. I hope you find the right person soon — and if I can point you in a useful direction, happy to do that.

[Your name]

---

## TEMPLATE 4 — Ending a retainer or ongoing engagement

**Subject: Notice — wrapping up our engagement**

Hi [Name],

As discussed, I'm giving [X days/30 days] notice that I'll be ending our engagement as of [specific date].

Between now and then:
- I'll complete [specific in-flight work]
- I'll make sure all files and access are transferred to you
- I'm happy to do a handoff call if that would be useful

It's been a real pleasure working together. [One genuine thing you appreciated about the engagement.]

Let's make sure the final [X weeks] are as smooth as possible.

[Your name]

---

## TEMPLATE 5 — Closing a prospect who said "not yet"

**Subject: Keeping this on file**

Hi [Name],

No problem at all — timing matters more than most people admit.

I'll keep your project in mind and circle back in [timeframe — be specific, e.g., "late Q3"]. If something comes up sooner on your end, just reach out.

Thanks for being straight with me — I appreciate it.

[Your name]

---

**Note:** Always end with something that keeps the door open unless there's a reason to close it completely. "Not now" is not "never." The follow-up in 3–6 months often converts — because you're the one who stayed professional when everyone else disappeared.
