# Kickoff Call Summary Template
### Send this within 24 hours of the kickoff call. It prevents misalignment before it starts.

---

**Subject: [Project Name] — kickoff summary**

Hi [Name],

Great call today. Here's a summary of what we covered so we're both working from the same page.

---

**Their goal (in their words):**
[Write this in their language, not yours. If they said "I need to stop losing clients the week after they sign," write that — not "improve client retention."]

**What success looks like:**
[Specific, measurable outcome you both agreed on]

---

**What I'm delivering:**

| Deliverable | Details |
|---|---|
| [Deliverable 1] | [Specifics — format, length, scope] |
| [Deliverable 2] | [Specifics] |
| [Deliverable 3] | [Specifics] |

**Not included in this scope:**
- [Thing 1 that was discussed but is out of scope]
- [Thing 2]

---

**Timeline:**

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Project start | [Date] |
| [Milestone 1] | [Date] |
| [Milestone 2] | [Date] |
| Final delivery | [Date] |

---

**What I need from you:**

| Item | Needed by |
|---|---|
| [Asset or info 1] | [Date] |
| [Asset or info 2] | [Date] |

---

**How we'll communicate:**
- Primary channel: [email / Slack / other]
- I'll send updates every [day of week]
- Best way to reach me for urgent items: [phone / email / Slack]
- My response window: [e.g., "within 4 hours on weekdays"]

**Revisions:**
This engagement includes [X] rounds of revisions. Changes after final approval are billed at $[rate]/hour.

---

**One thing I want to flag:**
[Optional — note anything from the call that could be a risk: unclear approval process, a tight timeline, a piece of information you still need, a concern the client mentioned]

---

If anything here is off or needs adjustment, let me know by [date] and we'll fix it before we start.

Otherwise, I'm heads-down and will have [first milestone] to you by [date].

[Your name]

---

## Why this matters

Most project problems come from two things: different expectations and delayed communication. This document eliminates most of the first. Sending it fast shows you're professional and paying attention. Most service providers never send this — and their clients notice.
