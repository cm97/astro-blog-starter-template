## How to read the responses

**Green signals:**
- Specific, complete answers throughout
- Budget aligns with what they're describing
- Has a real deadline (or honestly says they don't)
- Can describe what success looks like specifically

**Yellow signals:**
- Short answers on the hard questions (budget, deadline, what's gotten in the way)
- "Not sure" on budget with a complex scope
- Previous bad experience with another provider (dig into this on the call)

**Red signals:**
- Left budget blank
- Said "ASAP" as deadline with no date
- "I'll know it when I see it" as success criteria
- Previous provider "didn't deliver" but can't explain what was expected
- Multiple providers who "all let them down"

**If you see 2+ red signals:** This is not a bad person — but they may not be ready to invest in professional help, or they may have expectations that no one can meet. Either address it directly on the call or decline politely before the call.

---

## Notes on sending the form

- Send it before you book the call — not after
- If they don't fill it out within 48 hours, follow up once: "Just wanted to make sure you got the form — I need this before we connect."
- If they still don't fill it out, ask yourself why you're booking the call
