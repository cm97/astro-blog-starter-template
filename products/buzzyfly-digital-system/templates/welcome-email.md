# Welcome Email Templates
### Send the first one within 1 hour of payment. Speed = professionalism.

---

## TEMPLATE 1 — Standard welcome (service-based)

**Subject: Welcome — here's exactly what happens next**

Hi [Name],

Welcome aboard. I'm genuinely glad to be working together.

Here's what to expect in the next few days:

**What I need from you first:**
→ Please fill out this intake form before our kickoff call: [link]
It takes about 5 minutes and makes the kickoff call twice as useful.

**Your kickoff call:**
→ [Date and time]
→ [Meeting link or phone number]
→ We'll cover: your goals, the project timeline, and how we'll work together

**First deliverable:**
→ [What they can expect and when]

**How to reach me:**
→ Email: [your email] — I respond within [X hours] on weekdays
→ For urgent issues: [phone/other channel]

My office hours are [days and hours]. I'll always let you know if I'm heads-down on something.

Looking forward to it.

[Your name]

## The rule

Send the welcome email within 1 hour of payment. Every hour of delay increases buyer anxiety. The fastest way to lose a client you just won is to go silent right after they pay.
