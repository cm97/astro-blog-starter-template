## Phase 1 — Audit findings

### Technical SEO (public pages)
- **BaseHead.astro** sets title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph (og:title, og:description, og:image, og:site_name), Twitter cards, and robots noindex when requested. Defaults to branded 1200x630 OG image. Good.
- **robots.txt** disallows /admin, /app/, /api/, /thank-you, /success, /unsubscribe and points to sitemap-index.xml. Correct.
- **Sitemap** generated by @astrojs/sitemap. Filters private/post-checkout paths. Includes /jobs via customPages. Live sitemap serves 200.
- **Canonicals** set on every page via BaseHead.
- **Internal links**: Blog posts get 3 related posts by category + word overlap. Store and home link to each other. ProductCallout present on posts that set featuredProduct* fields.
- **Alt text**: Hero images on blog use empty alt when brand banner (hidden). Real images should have descriptive alt; most use placeholders.
- **Structured data**: Organization + WebSite on home. Product schema on /store. BlogPosting on posts.
- **Duplication**: Many redirects in astro.config.mjs consolidate old onboarding/follow-up/weekly-reset URLs. Good. No major duplicate title issues found on live pages sampled.
- **Gaps**: Some older posts may still lack seoTitle (title >60 chars). Free tools in public/tools/ and apps exist but were not fully audited for unique titles in this pass. No kids section found in public/.
