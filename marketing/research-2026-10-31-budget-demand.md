# Demand research: Buzzyfly Budget vs. the current coaching product (2026-10-31)

Sources: Google Trends via Firecrawl (US, 5 years and 12 months), Etsy search results, Gumroad sales data, price pages for competing apps. All numbers below come from those sources. Nothing is estimated.

## 1. Search demand, US, 5 years (Google Trends, 0–100 relative scale)

| Search term | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| budget app | 10.3 | 12.3 | 14.2 | 15.6 | 34.6 | 58.0 |
| budget template | 14.7 | 21.9 | 23.7 | 24.1 | 26.2 | 26.1 |
| invoice template | 22.6 | 28.6 | 27.9 | 25.4 | 26.1 | 26.0 |
| follow up email | 19.2 | 31.1 | 28.1 | 23.2 | 22.0 | 24.5 |
| **client onboarding** (current $49 product's niche) | 0.8 | 1.1 | 1.2 | 1.3 | 3.0 | 4.1 |

- "budget app" gets about **14 times** the search interest of "client onboarding" in 2026 (58.0 vs 4.1).
- "budget app" interest grew about 5x from 2021 to 2026. It peaked in July 2025. In the last 8 weeks it has run at 18–25, back near its long-run level, so the surge has cooled. Demand is still steady.
- "budget template" peaks in **January** (29.4 vs about 23 in other months). That's the New Year budgeting season, inside the 2-month window.

## 3. The competition charges a subscription. Buyers complain about it.

| App | Price |
|---|---|
| YNAB | $14.99/month or $109/year |
| Monarch | $14.99/month or $99.99/year |
| Rocket Money Premium | about $7–$14/month |

- Reddit threads compare these on price and look for "no subscription, one-time" options.
- **Buzzyfly Budget: $19 once, no subscription, no bank login.** That costs less than two months of YNAB.

## Verdict

- **The budget market is large and proven.** Search interest is about 14x the coaching niche, and thousands of buyers on Etsy have already paid.
- **Where Buzzyfly fits:** a real app for about the price of a good spreadsheet, and a one-time price against $100/year subscriptions.
- **Risks, stated plainly:**
  1. Competition is heavy: $1 Etsy sheets and free apps.
  2. Demand existing doesn't bring visitors to buzzyfly.com. Buyers shop on Etsy, Pinterest and TikTok, so the product has to be listed and promoted there.
  3. "budget app" search interest has come down from its 2025 peak.
